Reptomancer is an [[Elite]] enemy found exclusively in [[Act 3]].

{{Intents Table/row

{{Intents Table/row
| Name          = Snake Strike

| GivesToPlayer = Weak
}}
{{Intents Table/end}}

Always starts with {{Int|Reptomancer|Spawn Dagger}}.

Afterward it has an approximately equal chance of using {{Int|Reptomancer|Snake Strike}}, {{Int|Reptomancer|Big Bite}}, and {{Int|Reptomancer|Spawn Dagger}}.

Cannot use {{Int|Reptomancer|Snake Strike}} or {{Int|Reptomancer|Big Bite}} twice in a row. Cannot use {{Int|Reptomancer|Spawn Dagger}} three times in a row.

Only 4 {{M|Dagger|Daggers}} can be in play at any time. If {{Int|Reptomancer|Spawn Dagger}} was chosen with 4 {{M|Dagger|Daggers}} in play, it will use {{Int|Reptomancer|Snake Strike}} instead.
