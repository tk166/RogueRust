{{E|The Cleric}} is a {{QueryLink|Events|shrine:Yes|shrine Event}} found exclusively in [[Act 1]]. It is one of the events affected by {{Asc|15|Ascension}}. It can only be encountered if the player has at least 35 [[Gold]]; enough to afford at least one of the options.

== Options ==
{{Frame|Heal}} {{TS|gold||35 [[Gold]]:}} {{TS|positive||Heal X HP.}}
* X is equal to 25% of the player's Max HP, rounded down.

{{Frame|Purify}} {{TS|gold||50}} {{Asc|15|(75)}} {{TS|gold||[[Gold]]:}} {{TS|positive||Remove a card from your deck.}}

{{Frame|Leave}} Nothing happens.

== Dialogue ==
<blockquote>
A strange blue humanoid with a golden helm(?) approaches you with a huge smile.

{{TS||shake|"Hello friend!}} I am {{TS|blue||Cleric!}} Are you interested in my services?!" the creature shouts, loudly.

{{Frame|Heal}}
<blockquote><i>
A warm golden light envelops your body and dissipates.

The creature grins. "Cleric best healer. {{TS||shake|Have a good day!"}}
</i></blockquote>

{{Frame|Purify}}
<blockquote><i>
A cold blue flame envelops your body and dissipates.

The creature grins. "Cleric talented. {{TS||shake|Have a good day!"}}
</i></blockquote>

{{Frame|Leave}}
<blockquote><i>
You don't trust this {{TS|blue||"Cleric",}} so you leave.
</i></blockquote>
</blockquote>

== Trivia ==
* Cleric is also encountered in the {{E|Old Beggar}} event.
* {{R|Face of Cleric}} from the {{E|Face Trader}} event references Cleric. There is no special dialogue if the player encounters Cleric while owning his face.
