{{E|Bonfire Spirits}} is a shrine [[Event]] found in all [[Acts]].

Shrine events appear less frequently than other events.

== Options ==
{{Frame|Offer}} Receive a reward based on the offer.
* Offer a card in your deck to remove. The reward you receive depends on the rarity of the card you give.

== Dialogue ==
<blockquote>
<i>You happen upon what looks like a group of {{TS|purple||purple fire spirits}} {{TS||up|dancing}} around a large bonfire.</i>

Before selecting {{Frame|Offer}}
<blockquote>
<i>The spirits toss small bones and fragments into the fire, which {{TS||up|brilliantly erupts}} each time.</i><br>
<i>As you approach, the spirits all turn to you, expectantly...</i>
</blockquote>

{{Frame|Offer}}
<blockquote>
<i>You toss an offering into the bonfire.</i>

When offering a {{QueryLink|Cards|rarity:Basic|Basic}} card
<blockquote>
<i>Nothing happens...</i><br>
<i>The spirits seem to be ignoring you now. Disappointing...</i>
</blockquote>

When offering a {{QueryLink|Cards|rarity:Common|Common}} card
<blockquote>
<i>The flames grow slightly brighter.</i><br>
<i>The spirits continue dancing. You feel slightly warmer from their presence..</i><br>
<i>You {{TS|green||heal}} {{TS|blue||5}} HP.</i>
</blockquote>

When offering an {{QueryLink|Cards|rarity:Uncommon|Uncommon}} card
<blockquote>
<i>The flames erupt, growing significantly stronger!</i><br>
<i>The spirits dance around you excitedly, filling you with a {{TS||up|sense of warmth.}}</i><br>
<i>You are {{TS|green||healed}} to full HP.</i>
</blockquote>

When offering a {{QueryLink|Cards|rarity:Rare|Rare}} card
<blockquote>
<i>The flames {{TS||shake|burst}}, nearly knocking you off your feet, as the fire {{TS||shake|doubles}} in strength.</i><br>
<i>The spirits dance around you excitedly before {{TS||up|merging into your form,}} filling you with warmth and strength.</i><br>
<i>Your Max HP increases by {{TS|blue||10}} and you are {{TS|green||healed}} to full HP.</i>
</blockquote>

When offering a {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Curse|Curse}}
<blockquote>
<i>However, the spirits aren't happy you offered a {{TS|purple||Curse...}}</i><br>
<i>The card fizzles a meek black smoke. You receive a... {{TS|purple|up|something}} in return.</i><br>
(Gives {{R|Spirit Poop}})
</blockquote>
</blockquote>
</blockquote>

== Notes ==
* This event treats {{QueryLink|Cards|rarity:Basic|Basic}} cards as a lower rarity than {{QueryLink|Cards|rarity:Common|Common}}.
* Obtaining {{R|Spirit Poop}} adds the '''Poopy''' [[Score]] bonus which lowers your total score by one.
