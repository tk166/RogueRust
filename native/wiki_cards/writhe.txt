{{C|Writhe}} is an {{KW|Unplayable}} {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Curse|Curse}} Card. It is {{KW|Innate}}.

== Obtaining Writhe ==
Like most {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Curse|Curses}}, Writhe can be obtained from anything that gives a random Curse:
* Can be part of Neow's [[Third Blessing]] in exchange for a reward.
* {{E|Match and Keep}}.
* {{R|Cursed Key}}.
* {{KW|Transform|Transforming}} another Curse.

In addition, Writhe can be obtained from two events:
* {{E|The Mausoleum}} when choosing {{Frame|Open Coffin}}.
* {{E|Winding Halls}} when choosing {{Frame|Pass On}}.

== Interactions ==
=== Synergies ===
As Writhe is a '''{{QueryLink|Cards|type:Curse|Curse}}''', it interacts with the following:
* {{C|Fire Breathing}} will deal damage when it is drawn.
* {{R|Du-Vu Doll}} will give you 1 {{BD|Strength}} each combat.
* {{R|Blue Candle}} can be used to {{KW|Exhaust}} it for 1 HP. 
* When added to the deck, it will be negated by {{R|Omamori}}, or gain you 6 max HP if you have {{R|Darkstone Periapt}} and ''not'' {{R|Omamori}}.

As Writhe is '''{{KW|Innate}}''', the downside of having it can be mitigated:
* {{R|Gambling Chip}} allows you to discard it away at the start of combat, meaning you won't draw it until after the first shuffle.
* {{R|Ring of the Snake}} and {{R|Bag of Preparation}} both draw you additional cards on your first turn, lessening the impact of an Innate curse in your starting hand.

=== Anti-Synergies ===
* As {{C|Clash}} can only be played when all cards in your hand are {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Attack|Attacks}}, if it is drawn at the same time as Writhe, it cannot be played.

== Trivia ==
As most Curses are only given in a single Event (not counting random ones), Writhe is tied with {{C|Parasite}} and {{C|Regret}} for being the Curse with the most ways to get it.
