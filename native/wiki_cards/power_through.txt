{{C|Power Through}} is a 1 {{Icon|RE}} cost {{QueryLink|Cards|color:Red|Ironclad}} {{QueryLink|Cards|rarity:Uncommon|Uncommon}} {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Skill|Skill}} Card. It gives 15 (20 if upgraded) {{KW|Block}}, and adds 2 {{C|Wound|Wounds}} into your hand.

== Interactions ==
=== Synergies ===
* {{C|Power Through}} is one of the few cards that increases the amount of cards in hand without drawing from your own deck, and therefore synergizes with {{C|Second Wind}} and {{C|Fiend Fire}}, as well as any other card that requires fodder to {{KW|Exhaust}}.

* Synergizes with {{C|Fire Breathing}} and {{C|Evolve}}, as it creates {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Status|Status}} cards. However, the {{C|Wound|Wounds}} get created in your hand, meaning it can take some time before they can be drawn to trigger these effects.

=== Neutral ===
* If a {{C|Wound}} generated by this card exceeds your hand size limit, it gets added to the discard pile.
