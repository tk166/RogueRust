{{C|Headbutt}} is a 1 {{Icon|RE}}-cost {{QueryLink|Cards|color:Red|Ironclad}} {{QueryLink|Cards|rarity:Common|Common}} {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Attack|Attack}} Card. It deals 9 (12 if upgraded) damage, and allows you to select one card in your discard pile to place on top of your draw pile.

== Interactions ==
=== Order ===
* Although {{C|Mayhem}} plays the top card of the draw pile, it cannot be used in tandem with {{C|Headbutt}} to play a specific card, as {{C|Mayhem}} activates ''after'' cards are drawn at the start of your turn, meaning the card will already be in hand.
** This could still be achieved if you used {{C|Headbutt}} on more cards than you draw at the start of your turn. 

=== Other ===
* If there is only one card in your discard pile, it will automatically be placed on top of your draw pile.
