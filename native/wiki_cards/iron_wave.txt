{{C|Iron Wave}} is a 1 {{Icon|RE}} cost {{QueryLink|Cards|color:Red|Ironclad}} {{QueryLink|Cards|rarity:Common|Common}} {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Attack|Attack}} Card. It gives 5 (7 if upgraded) {{KW|Block}}, and deals 5 (7 if upgraded) damage.

== Interactions ==
=== Order ===
* The {{KW|Block}} is gained before the damage is dealt or the card is resolved, meaning the {{KW|Block}} already protects from any damage sustained as a result of playing this card through effects like:
** {{BD|Sharp Hide}} or {{BD|Thorns}}.
** {{BD|Beat of Death}}.

=== Neutral ===
* Both values are affected separately by effects that change the amount of damage and {{KW|Block}}, such as {{BD|Strength}} and {{BD|Dexterity}}.
