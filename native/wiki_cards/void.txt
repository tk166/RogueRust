{{C|Void}} is an {{KW|Unplayable}} {{QueryLink|Cards|color:Colorless|Colorless}} {{QueryLink|Cards|Common:Common|Common}} {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Status|Status}} Card. Whenever this card is drawn, lose 1 {{Icon|CE}} '''Energy'''.

== Obtaining Void ==
Playing {{C|TURBO}} will add a {{C|Void}} to the player's discard pile.

The following enemies can give the player {{C|Void}}:
* {{M|Awakened One}}
** {{Int|Awakened One|Sludge}} - Adds a {{C|Void}} into your draw pile.
* {{M|Corrupt Heart}}
** {{Int|Corrupt Heart|Debilitate}} - Adds 1 {{C|Void}} into your draw pile.

== Interactions ==

=== Synergies ===
* Triggers the damage from {{C|Fire Breathing}}.
* Draws cards with {{C|Evolve}}.
* Is {{KW|Exhaust|Exhausted}} by {{C|Second Wind}} and triggers its {{KW|Block}} gain.
* Can be {{KW|Exhaust|Exhausted}} with {{R|Medical Kit}} to trigger exhaust synergies such as {{C|Feel No Pain}} or {{R|Charon's Ashes}}, or will {{KW|Exhaust}} on its own at the end of your turn.
* Energy is only lost when the card is drawn. If the card is added to your hand by other means, such as with {{C|Seek}}, {{C|Hologram}}, or {{P|Liquid Memories}}, the energy reduction can be evaded.

=== Neutral ===
* Is {{KW|Exhaust|Exhausted}} by {{C|Sever Soul}} and {{C|Second Wind}}, since it is not an {{QueryLink|Cards|Type:Attack|Attack}} card.

=== Anti-Synergies ===
* Prevents {{C|Clash}} from being played.
