{{C|Dark Shackles}} is a 0 {{Icon|CE}}-cost {{QueryLink|Cards|color:Colorless|Colorless}} {{QueryLink|Cards|rarity:Uncommon|Uncommon}} {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Skill|Skill}} Card. It applies '''negative''' 9 (15 if upgraded) {{BD|Strength}} to the target, and gives them the same amount of {{BD|Shackled}} to regain that missing {{BD|Strength}} at the end of their turn. {{C|Dark Shackles}} will then {{KW|Exhaust}} itself.

== Obtaining Dark Shackles ==
{{C|Dark Shackles}} is a {{QueryLink|Cards|color:Colorless|Colorless}} card, and therefore can be obtained in the following ways:
* Can be bought from the [[Merchant]].
* Can appear in the {{E|Sensory Stone}} event, from a choice of 3-9 {{QueryLink|Cards|color:Colorless|Colorless}} cards, depending on the option that is selected.
* Can be obtained from Neow's [[First Blessing]] via the {{TS|green||'''Obtain an Uncommon Colorless Card'''}} bonus.
* Can be obtained from [[Monsters]] and [[Elites]] with {{R|Prismatic Shard}}.
** Also found in the [[Custom Mode|Daily Climb]] with the {{KW|Diverse}} or {{KW|Colorless Cards}} modifiers.
* Can be obtained from the {{Frame|Success?}} option at {{E|Knowing Skull}}.

== Interactions ==
=== Neutral ===
* The {{M|The Champ}}, the {{M|Awakened One}} and the {{M|Time Eater}} clear [[Debuff]s] when they are about to enter their second phase, meaning the {{BD|Strength}} penalty is maintained while {{BD|Shackled}} is removed. This means they get the {{BD|Strength}} penalty permanently instead of just that turn.
** However, if this causes them to have '''negative''' {{BD|Strength}}, this will also be considered a [[Debuff]] and will be removed, leaving them with 0 {{BD|Strength}}.
* The {{M|Corrupt Heart}} clears ''only'' '''negative''' {{BD|Strength}} when they buff (except for the turn 1 buff), meaning the {{BD|Strength}} penalty is removed but the {{BD|Shackled}} is maintained. This means it will permanently gain that amount of {{BD|Strength}} at the end of their turn.
* {{C|Dark Shackles}} applies two effects per enemy: a [[Debuff]] to remove the {{BD|Strength}}, and {{BD|Shackled}} to add it back at end of turn. Despite this, enemies with {{BD|Artifact}} will still just lose one stack to negate the negative {{BD|Strength}}, without receiving the {{BD|Shackled}}.
