{{C|Offering}} is a 0 {{Icon|RE}}-cost {{QueryLink|Cards|color:Red|Ironclad}} {{QueryLink|Cards|rarity:Rare|Rare}} {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Skill|Skill}} Card. It costs 6 HP to play, gives 2 {{Icon|RE}} energy and draws 3 (5 if upgraded) cards.

== Interactions ==
=== Synergies ===
* {{C|Offering}} can be used to trigger {{C|Rupture}}, {{R|Runic Cube}} and {{R|Self-Forming Clay}} and {{R|Runic Cube}}, and lower the cost of {{C|Blood for Blood}}.

=== Neutral ===
* {{C|Offering}} can trigger {{R|Centennial Puzzle}}, which could be wasteful, as the combined total draw can exceed the hand size limit.
