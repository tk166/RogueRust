{{C|Wound}} is an {{KW|Unplayable}} {{QueryLink|Cards|color:Colorless|Colorless}} {{QueryLink|Cards|rarity:Common|Common}} {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Status|Status}} Card.

== Obtaining Wound ==

* {{C|Wild Strike}} adds a Wound to the player's draw pile.
* {{C|Power Through}} adds 2 Wounds to the player's hand. If one or more Wounds can't fit in the player's hand (i.e. they have 9 or 10 cards in hand already), the extra Wound(s) are added to the discard pile instead.
* {{R|Mark of Pain}} adds 2 Wounds to the player's draw pile after they've finished drawing their opening hand for the start of combat.

The following enemies can give the player Wounds:
* {{M|Book of Stabbing}}
** {{BD|Painful Stabs}} - Whenever you receive attack damage from this enemy, add a {{C|Wound}} into your discard pile.
* {{M|Taskmaster}}
** {{Int|Taskmaster|Scouring Whip}} - Adds 1({{Asc|3|2}} | {{Asc|18|3}}) {{C|Wound|Wounds}} into your draw pile.
* {{M|Dagger}}
** {{Int|Dagger|Wound}} - Adds a {{C|Wound}} into your discard pile.
* {{M|Corrupt Heart}}
** Gains {{BD|Painful Stabs}} every 5th time using {{Int|Corrupt Heart|Buff}} starting at the 3rd use.
** {{Int|Corrupt Heart|Debilitate}} - Adds 1 {{C|Wound}} into your draw pile.

== Interactions ==
=== Order ===
* {{R|Mark of Pain}} adds 2 Wounds to the player's draw pile after they've finished drawing their normal 5-card opening hand for the start of combat.
** These Wounds '''can''' still end up in the opening hand due to {{R|Bag of Preparation}} drawing extra cards at the start of combat, as long as it was picked up after already having obtained {{R|Mark of Pain}} earlier.

=== Synergies ===
* Triggers the damage from {{C|Fire Breathing}}.
* Draws cards with {{C|Evolve}}.
* Is {{KW|Exhaust|exhausted}} by {{C|Second Wind}} and triggers its {{KW|Block}} gain.
* Can be {{KW|Exhaust|exhausted}} with {{R|Medical Kit}} to trigger exhaust synergies such as {{C|Feel No Pain}} or {{R|Charon's Ashes}}.

=== Neutral ===
* Is {{KW|Exhaust|exhausted}} by {{C|Sever Soul}} since it's not an {{QueryLink|Cards|Type:Attack|Attack}}.

=== Anti-Synergies ===
* Prevents {{C|Clash}} from being played.

== Trivia ==
* This card's [[Beta Art]] shows the [[Silent]] having a very understandable reaction to a minor cut.
