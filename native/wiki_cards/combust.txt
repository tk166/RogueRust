{{C|Combust}} is a 1 {{Icon|RE}}-cost {{QueryLink|Cards|color:Red|Ironclad}} {{QueryLink|Cards|rarity:Uncommon|Uncommon}} {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Power|Power}} Card. It gives 5 (7 if upgraded) {{BD|Combust}}, which deals that amount of damage to '''ALL''' enemies and costs you 1 HP every at the end of your turn.

Playing {{C|Combust}} multiple times will increase the amount of {{BD|Combust}} and will increase the HP loss of the entire stack of {{BD|Combust}} by 1, for every copy played.

== Interactions ==
=== Synergies ===
* {{R|Tungsten Rod}} completely negates the damage from the first play of {{C|Combust}}.
* {{C|Combust}} can be used to reliably and repeatedly trigger {{C|Rupture}} and {{R|Self-Forming Clay}}, and lower the cost of {{C|Blood for Blood}}.

=== Anti-Synergies ===
* Since {{BD|Combust}} activates after you end your turn but before you discard your hand, any draw as a result of {{BD|Combust}}'s HP loss in combination with {{R|Runic Cube}} or {{R|Centennial Puzzle}} will be wasted.
