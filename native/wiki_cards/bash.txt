{{C|Bash}} is a 2 {{Icon|RE}}-cost {{QueryLink|Cards|color:Red|Ironclad}} {{QueryLink|Cards|rarity:Basic|Basic}} {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Attack|Attack}} Card. It deals 8 (10 if upgraded) damage and applies 2 (3 if upgraded) {{BD|Vulnerable}} to the target.

== Obtaining Bash ==
The [[Ironclad]] starts the game with one copy of {{C|Bash}} in his deck. It cannot be obtained through regular card rewards, but can be obtained in the {{E|Match and Keep}} Event.
