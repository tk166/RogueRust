{{C|Exhume}} is a 1 (0 if upgraded) {{Icon|RE}}-cost {{QueryLink|Cards|color:Red|Ironclad}} {{QueryLink|Cards|rarity:Rare|Rare}} {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Skill|Skill}} Card. It puts a previously {{KW|Exhaust|Exhausted}} card back in your hand. {{C|Exhume}} will then {{KW|Exhaust}} itself.

== Interactions ==
=== Synergy ===
* As {{C|Exhume}} {{KW|Exhaust|Exhausts}} itself and gives back another card which was already {{KW|Exhaust|Exhausted}}, it has {{KW|Exhaust}}-synergy with {{C|Dark Embrace}}, {{C|Feel No Pain}}, {{R|Charon's Ashes}}, and {{R|Dead Branch}}.

* {{C|Exhume}} is most useful for its flexibity, as it can allow an additional play of any card that was previously {{KW|Exhaust|Exhausted}}, effectively acting as a copy of whatever {{KW|Exhaust|Exhausted}} card is most useful at the time.
** In the case of cards that scale themselves, such as {{C|Ritual Dagger}} or {{C|Rampage}}, {{C|Exhume}} is better than a second copy of that card, as it allows you to benefit from the previous scaling of that card, and scale that exact same card again.

=== Neutral ===
* {{C|Exhume}} cannot be used to put another {{KW|Exhaust|Exhausted}} {{C|Exhume}} back in your hand.
** This is done to prevent degenerate strategies that would be too easy to create an infinite damage combo. For example, by repeatedly using {{C|Exhume}}+ to bring back another {{C|Exhume}}+ and infinitely trigger the damage from {{R|Letter Opener}} without spending any {{KW|Energy}} or needing to draw cards.

== Trivia ==
* This card is the only way to bring back an {{KW|Exhaust|Exhausted}} card.
