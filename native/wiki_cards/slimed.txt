{{C|Slimed}} is a 1 {{Icon|CE}}-cost {{QueryLink|Cards|color:Colorless|Colorless}} {{QueryLink|Cards|rarity:Common|Common}} {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Status|Status}} Card. Slimed is the ONLY playable {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Status|Status}} card.

== Obtaining Slimed ==
The following enemies can give the player {{C|Slimed}}:
* {{M|Spike Slime (L)}}
** {{Int|Spike Slime (L)|Flame Tackle}} - Adds 2 {{C|Slimed}} into your discard pile.
* {{M|Spike Slime (M)}}
** {{Int|Spike Slime (M)|Flame Tackle}} - Adds a {{C|Slimed}} into your discard pile.
* {{M|Acid Slime (L)}}
** {{Int|Acid Slime (L)|Corrosive Spit}} - Adds 2 {{C|Slimed}} into your discard pile.
* {{M|Acid Slime (M)}}
** {{Int|Acid Slime (M)|Corrosive Spit}} - Adds a {{C|Slimed}} into your discard pile.
* {{M|Slime Boss}}
** {{Int|Slime Boss|Goop Spray}} - Adds 3({{Asc|19|5}}) {{C|Slimed}} into your discard pile.
* {{M|Time Eater}} '''Only above {{Asc|19|Ascension}}.'''
** {{Int|Time Eater|Head Slam}} - Adds 2 {{C|Slimed}} into your discard pile.
* {{M|Corrupt Heart}}
** {{Int|Corrupt Heart|Debilitate}} - Adds 1 {{C|Slimed}} into your draw pile.

== Interactions ==
=== Synergies ===
* Triggers the damage from {{C|Fire Breathing}}.
* Draws cards with {{C|Evolve}}.
* Is {{KW|Exhaust|exhausted}} by {{C|Second Wind}} and triggers its {{KW|Block}} gain.
* Can be {{KW|Exhaust|exhausted}} to trigger exhaust synergies such as {{C|Feel No Pain}} or {{R|Charon's Ashes}}.

=== Neutral ===
* Is {{KW|Exhaust|exhausted}} by {{C|Sever Soul}} since it's not an {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Attack|Attack}}.

=== Anti-Synergies ===
* Prevents {{C|Clash}} from being played.
