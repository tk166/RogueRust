{{C|Juggernaut}} is a 2 {{Icon|RE}}-cost {{QueryLink|Cards|color:Red|Ironclad}} {{QueryLink|Cards|rarity:Rare|Rare}} {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Power|Power}} Card. It grants 5 (7 if upgraded) {{BD|Juggernaut}}, which deals damage equal to that amount whenever you gain {{KW|Block}}.

== Interactions ==
=== Synergies ===
* Synergizes with any source of block, but particularly with block that can activate multiple times, such as {{C|Rage}}, {{C|Second Wind}}, {{C|Feel No Pain}}, {{R|Ornamental Fan}} or {{R|The Abacus}}.

=== Neutral ===
* Juggernaut will only be triggered by gaining separate instances of {{KW|Block}}. This means that {{BD|Metallicize}}, {{BD|Plated Armor}} and {{BD|Next Turn Block}} will only trigger it only once each regardless of how many different sources they have.
** For example, playing two {{C|Metallicize}} cards will still result in only one trigger of {{BD|Juggernaut}} per turn.
