{{C|Anger}} is a 0 {{Icon|RE}}-cost {{QueryLink|Cards|color:Red|Ironclad}} {{QueryLink|Cards|rarity:Common|Common}} {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Attack|Attack}} Card. It deals 6 (8 if upgraded) damage, and adds a copy of itself into your discard pile.

== Interactions ==
=== Synergies ===
* {{C|Anger}} works well with any draw, most notably with {{R|Unceasing Top}}.
* The created copy will be upgraded if the original copy was upgraded, or if {{C|Master Reality}} is active.
* Playing it multiple times, through an effect such as {{C|Double Tap}} or a {{P|Duplication Potion}}, will make multiple copies as well.
