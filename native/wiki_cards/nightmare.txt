{{C|Nightmare}} is a 3 (2 if upgraded) {{Icon|GE}}-cost {{QueryLink|Cards|color:Green|Silent}} {{QueryLink|Cards|rarity:Rare|Rare}} {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Skill|Skill}} Card.

== Interactions ==
=== Neutral ===
* When a card is copied, any alterations to that card are also copied, where the duration of these alterations is the same for the copy as for the original. After being copied, these cards behave fully independently. The alterations that are copied include but are not limited to:
** A changed energy cost through something like {{R|Snecko Eye}}, {{C|Madness}} or {{P|Liquid Memories}}, or the automatic energy change of cards like {{C|Blood for Blood}} and {{C|Masterful Stab}}.
** Changes to the values of the card, such as upgrades to {{C|Searing Blow}} or increased damage of {{C|Windmill Strike}}.
* Changes that last for one turn, such as an energy discount through {{C|Bullet Time}} or the energy discount of {{C|Eviscerate}} are not copied.

* If a copy created by this exceeds the hand size limit, it gets added to the discard pile.
