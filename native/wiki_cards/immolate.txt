{{Small Card Infobox|Burn}}{{C|Immolate}} is a 2 {{Icon|RE}}-cost {{QueryLink|Cards|color:Red|Ironclad}} {{QueryLink|Cards|rarity:Rare|Rare}} {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Attack|Attack}} Card. It deals 21 (28 if upgraded) to '''ALL''' enemies and adds a {{C|Burn}} into your discard pile.

== Interactions ==
=== Order === 
* The {{C|Burn}} gets added into your discard pile ''before'' triggering {{R|Ink Bottle}}, meaning it can be drawn if your draw pile is empty.

=== Synergies ===
* Synergizes with {{C|Fire Breathing}} and {{C|Evolve}}, as it creates a {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Status|Status}} card in your discard pile. However, as it gets created in your discard pile, it can take some time before it can be drawn to trigger these effects.

=== Anti-Synergies ===
* Hexaghost can upgrade all {{C|Burn|Burns}} in the draw and discard pile, meaning it will also upgrade {{C|Burn|Burns}} generated by this card.
