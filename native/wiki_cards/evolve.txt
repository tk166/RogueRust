{{C|Evolve}} is a 1 {{Icon|RE}}-cost {{QueryLink|Cards|color:Red|Ironclad}} {{QueryLink|Cards|rarity:Uncommon|Uncommon}} {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Power|Power}} Card. It gives 1 (2 if upgraded) {{BD|Evolve}}, which draws that amount of cards whenever a {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Status|Status}} card is drawn.

== Interactions ==
=== Neutral ===
* {{C|Evolve}} only activates upon {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Status|Status}} cards being drawn. This means that cards created in hand, such as through {{C|Power Through}}, do not trigger it.
