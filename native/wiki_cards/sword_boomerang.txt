{{C|Sword Boomerang}} is a 1 {{Icon|RE}}-cost {{QueryLink|Cards|color:Red|Ironclad}} {{QueryLink|Cards|rarity:Common|Common}} {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Attack|Attack}} Card. It deals 3 damage 3 (4 if upgraded) times, each time to a random enemy. 

== Interactions ==
=== Synergies ===
* Benefits 3 (4 if upgraded) times from {{BD|Strength}} or {{BD|Vigor}}.
* As it is a multi-hit attack, it can remove several stacks of {{BD|Malleable}} or {{BD|Flying}} at once.
