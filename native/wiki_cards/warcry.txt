{{C|Warcry}} is a 0 {{Icon|RE}}-cost {{QueryLink|Cards|color:Red|Ironclad}} {{QueryLink|Cards|rarity:Common|Common}} {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Skill|Skill}} Card. It draws 1 card (2 cards if upgraded) and allows you to select one card in hand to place on top of your drawpile. {{C|Warcry}} will then {{KW|Exhaust}} itself.

== Interactions ==
=== Order ===
* Selecting the card to put on top of your draw pile happens ''after'' drawing, meaning a card drawn this way can potentially be placed right back on top.

* Although {{C|Mayhem}} plays the top card of the draw pile, it cannot be used in tandem with {{C|Warcry}} to play a specific card, as {{C|Mayhem}} activates ''after'' cards are drawn at the start of your turn, meaning the card will already be in hand.
