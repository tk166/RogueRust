== Interactions ==
=== Synergies ===
* Upgrading this card in combat will increase its damage for the remainder of combat. This can be done through:

==== Cards ====
* {{C|Armaments}} can upgrade it multiple times over the course of a battle.
* {{C|Apotheosis}}
* {{C|Master Reality}}, if {{C|Searing Blow}} is generated in combat. 

==== Relics and potions ====
* {{R|Warped Tongs}} can upgrade it multiple times over the course of a battle.
* {{P|Blessing of the Forge}}

== Bugs ==
* If {{C|Searing Blow}} is generated with {{BD|Master Reality}} active, it will generate with +2 {{KW|Upgrade|Upgrades}} instead of +1, unless generated by {{C|Foreign Influence}} or {{R|Nilry's Codex}}. 
** This can be replicated without {{R|Prismatic Shard}} or {{E|A Note For Yourself}}, by generating {{C|Searing Blow}} with {{C|Foreign Influence}} as [[The Watcher]] in the [[Bronze Automaton]] combat. Allowing one of its {{BD|Minion|Minions}} to steal {{C|Searing Blow}} and then retrieving it by killing that {{BD|Minion}} counts as that {{C|Searing Blow}} being generated.
*** This can be used to achieve a maximum of +5 {{KW|Upgrade|Upgrades}}: +1 through initial generation via {{C|Foreign Influence}}, and +2 through both {{BD|Minion|Minions}}.
