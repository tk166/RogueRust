{{C|Spot Weakness}} is a 1 {{Icon|RE}} cost {{QueryLink|Cards|color:Red|Ironclad}} {{QueryLink|Cards|rarity:Uncommon|Uncommon}} {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Skill|Skill}} Card. It gives 3 (4 if upgraded) {{BD|Strength}} if the target's [[Intent]] is to attack.

== Interactions ==
=== Neutral ===
* When [[Intent|Intents]] are obscured by {{R|Runic Dome}}, it can be harder to know if this card will have an effect. However, {{C|Spot Weakness}} can still be played on a target, and this can be used to determine if the target is attacking when you are unsure.

* It does not matter if the target's attack has an additional effect or deals 0 damage, as long as attacking is part of the [[Intent]], this card will activate its effects.
