{{C|Infernal Blade}} is a 1 (0 if upgraded) {{Icon|RE}} cost {{QueryLink|Cards|color:Red|Ironclad}} {{QueryLink|Cards|rarity:Uncommon|Uncommon}} {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Skill|Skill}} Card. It will generate a random {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Attack|Attack}} card, which will have its normal cost replaced by 0 for this turn. {{C|Infernal Blade}} will then {{KW|Exhaust}} itself. X-cost {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Attack|Attack}} cards generated this way will still consume all your energy. 

{{C|Infernal Blade}} can only generate cards you can find in card rewards through character choice or custom run modifiers, and is not affected by {{R|Prismatic Shard}}.
