{{C|Fiend Fire}} is a 2 {{Icon|RE}}-cost {{QueryLink|Cards|color:Red|Ironclad}} {{QueryLink|Cards|rarity:Rare|Rare}} {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Attack|Attack}} Card. It {{KW|Exhaust|Exhausts}} all cards in your hand, and deals 7 (10 if upgraded) damage for every card {{KW|Exhaust|Exhausted}} this way, excluding itself. {{C|Fiend Fire}} will then {{KW|Exhaust}} itself.

== Interactions ==
=== Order ===
* If you have {{R|Dead Branch}} and {{C|Dark Embrace}} and play {{C|Fiend Fire}} to {{KW|Exhaust|Exhausts}} multiple cards, it resolves 1 card at a time. This means the order goes: 
# Your hand is fully {{KW|Exhaust|Exhausted}}.
# The 1st {{KW|Exhaust|Exhausted}} card generates a new card via {{R|Dead Branch}}.
# The 1st {{KW|Exhaust|Exhausted}} card draws a card via {{C|Dark Embrace}}.
# Moves onto the 2nd card, repeat steps 2 and 3.

=== Synergies ===
* {{C|Fiend Fire}} has {{KW|Exhaust}}-synergy with {{C|Dark Embrace}}, {{C|Feel No Pain}}, {{R|Charon's Ashes}} and {{R|Dead Branch}}, and can reliably {{KW|Exhaust}} {{C|Sentinel}}.
* Benefits once from {{BD|Strength}} or {{BD|Vigor}} per hit, so per card {{KW|Exhaust|Exhausted}}.

=== Neutral === 
* {{C|Fiend Fire}} only has an effect if it has cards to {{KW|Exhaust}}. This means that playing it twice using for example {{C|Double Tap}} or {{R|Necronomicon}} does nothing, unless the cards are replaced in between through {{C|Dark Embrace}} or {{R|Dead Branch}}.

=== Trivia ===
* {{C|Fiend Fire}} appears as a cameo in [https://foursouls.com/cards/rwz-fiend_fire/ The Binding of Isaac: Four Souls].
