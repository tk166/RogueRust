{{C|Berserk}} is a 0 {{Icon|RE}}-cost {{QueryLink|Cards|color:Red|Ironclad}} {{QueryLink|Cards|rarity:Rare|Rare}} {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Power|Power}} Card. It gives 1 {{BD|Berserk}}, which gives that amount of {{Icon|RE}} '''Energy''' at the start of every turn. It debuffs you with 2 (1 if upgraded) {{BD|Vulnerable}}.

== Interactions ==
=== Synergies ===
* {{R|Odd Mushroom}} reduces the severity of the {{BD|Vulnerable}} you get from this card.
* {{R|Orange Pellets}} can remove the {{BD|Vulnerable}} you get from this card.
* Sources of {{BD|Artifact}}, such as {{R|Clockwork Souvenir}}, {{P|Ancient Potion}}, {{C|Core Surge}} and {{C|Panacea}} can prevent this card from applying {{BD|Vulnerable}}.
