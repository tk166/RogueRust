{{C|Dark Embrace}} is a 2 (1 if upgraded) {{Icon|RE}}-cost {{QueryLink|Cards|color:Red|Ironclad}} {{QueryLink|Cards|rarity:Uncommon|Uncommon}} {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Power|Power}} Card. It gives 1 {{BD|Dark Embrace}}, which draws 1 card every time a card is {{KW|Exhaust|Exhausted}}.

== Interactions ==
=== Order ===
* When playing a card that {{KW|Exhaust|Exhausts}} a card, {{BD|Dark Embrace}} draws ''after'' the played card is resolved. If the played card draws or adds cards to your hand, that will happen ''before'' {{BD|Dark Embrace}} is triggered. 
** For example, when bringing a card back with {{C|Exhume}}, it will place that card in your hand first, and only then draw cards through {{BD|Dark Embrace}}. This means that if your hand is full when playing that {{C|Exhume}}, it will still function as normal, and the draw of {{BD|Dark Embrace}} will be wasted.

* {{KW|Ethereal}} cards {{KW|Exhaust}} at the end of your turn, but cards drawn through {{BD|Dark Embrace}} this way are not discarded, effectively giving you extra cards in hand next turn.
