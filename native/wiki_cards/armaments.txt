{{C|Armaments}} is a 1 {{Icon|RE}}-cost {{QueryLink|Cards|color:Red|Ironclad}} {{QueryLink|Cards|rarity:Common|Common}} {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Skill|Skill}} Card. It gives 5 {{KW|Block}}, and allows you to select one card in your hand to upgrade. If {{C|Armaments}} is upgraded, it upgrades your entire hand instead. These upgrades last until the end of combat.

== Interactions ==
=== Synergies ===
* Can repeatedly upgrade {{C|Searing Blow}} for the duration of combat.

=== Neutral ===
* Using {{C|Armaments}} to {{KW|Upgrade}} a card that changes {{Icon|RE}}-cost when {{KW|Upgrade|Upgraded}} will immediately set it to that {{Icon|RE}}-cost. This also applies if the card previously had its cost randomized by {{BD|Confused}} or {{P|Snecko Oil}}, unless its cost initially got randomized to 0, in which case it remains 0 when upgraded.
** A {{C|Blood for Blood}} that costs more than 0 {{Icon|RE}} '''Energy''' will always go down by 1 in {{Icon|RE}}-cost when {{KW|Upgrade|Upgraded}}, regardless of what cost it was randomized to.
** For example, if an unupgraded {{C|Corruption}} is randomized to 1 {{Icon|RE}}-cost, upgrading it with {{C|Armaments}} will increase its cost to 2.

* Although {{C|Burn}} has an upgraded version, it cannot be upgraded by the player, and is therefore unaffected by {{C|Armaments}}.

=== Anti-Synergies ===
* Having played {{C|Apotheosis}} or having your hand {{KW|Upgrade|Upgraded}} by {{P|Blessing of the Forge}} completely removes the second effect of {{C|Armaments}} for respectively that combat or that hand. Similarly, other sources of {{KW|Upgrade|Upgrades}}, such as {{R|Warped Tongs}} and {{C|Lesson Learned}} can reduce the effectiveness of this card.
** Having {{C|Searing Blow}} causes an exception to this, as it can still be {{KW|Upgrade|Upgraded}} by {{C|Armaments}} even if {{KW|Upgrade|Upgraded}} before.
