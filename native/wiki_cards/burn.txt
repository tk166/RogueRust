{{C|Burn}} is an {{KW|Unplayable}} {{QueryLink|Cards|color:Colorless|Colorless}} {{QueryLink|Cards|Common:Common|Common}} {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Status|Status}} Card. Burn is the ONLY status card that can be {{KW|Upgrade|Upgraded}}.

== Obtaining Burn ==
Playing {{C|Immolate}} or {{C|Overclock}} will add a Burn to the player's discard pile.

The following enemies can give the player Burns:
* {{M|Hexaghost}}
** {{Int|Hexaghost|Sear}} - Adds 1({{Asc|19|2}}) {{C|Burn|Burns}} into your discard pile.
** {{Int|Hexaghost|Inferno}} - Adds 3 {{C|Burn|Burns+}} into your discard pile. Upgrades all {{C|Burn|Burns}}. For the rest of combat, any {{C|Burn|Burns}} created by {{Int|Hexaghost|Sear}} will be upgraded.
* {{M|Orb Walker}}
** {{Int|Orb Walker|Laser}} - Adds a {{C|Burn}} into your draw pile, and another {{C|Burn}} into your discard pile.
* {{M|Nemesis}}
** {{Int|Nemesis|Tri Burn}} - Adds 3({{Asc|18|5}}) {{C|Burn|Burns}} into your discard pile.
* {{M|Spire Spear}}
** {{Int|Spire Spear|Burn Strike}} - Adds 2 {{C|Burn|Burns}} into your discard pile({{Asc|18|on top of your draw pile}}).
* {{M|Corrupt Heart}}
** {{Int|Corrupt Heart|Debilitate}} - Adds 1 {{C|Burn}} into your draw pile.

== Interactions ==

=== Synergies ===

* If the player loses health due to unblocked damage from Burn while {{C|Rupture}} is in play, the damage from Burn will trigger Rupture's {{BD|Strength}} gain.
* If the player loses health due to unblocked damage from Burn while possessing {{R|Self-Forming Clay}}, the damage from Burn will trigger Self-Forming Clay's {{KW|Block}} gain next turn.
* Triggers the damage from {{C|Fire Breathing}}.
* Draws cards with {{C|Evolve}}.
* Is {{KW|Exhaust|exhausted}} by {{C|Second Wind}} and triggers its {{KW|Block}} gain.
* Can be {{KW|Exhaust|exhausted}} with {{R|Medical Kit}} to trigger exhaust synergies such as {{C|Feel No Pain}} or {{R|Charon's Ashes}}.

=== Neutral ===

* Burn will contribute 1 {{KW|Block}} gained for {{R|Cloak Clasp}} as the relic triggers before Burn is discarded. This is overall a block penalty due to Burn's damage.
* Burn's unblocked damage will be reduced by {{R|Tungsten Rod}}.
* Burn will still be discarded after dealing damage at the end of the turn if the player has {{R|Runic Pyramid}} or {{KW|Retain|retains}} it using {{C|Equilibrium}}, {{C|Well-Laid Plans}}, or {{C|Meditate}}.
* Is {{KW|Exhaust|exhausted}} by {{C|Sever Soul}} since it's not an {{QueryLink|Cards|Type:Attack|Attack}}.

=== Anti-Synergies ===

* If the player loses health due to unblocked damage from {{C|Burn}} while possessing {{R|Centennial Puzzle}} or {{R|Runic Cube}}, the drawn card(s) will be discarded with the rest of their hand.
* Prevents {{C|Clash}} from being played.

== Trivia ==

* Burn is the only {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Status|Status}} card that has an upgraded version.
** The only way to obtain a {{C|Burn}}+ is from the {{M|Hexaghost}} converting all Burns in the player's hand, draw pile, and discard pile with Burn+ following their ''Inferno'' attack. Additionally, after the first time ''Inferno'' is used, all of Hexaghost's attacks that would normally give the player a Burn will give the player a Burn+ instead.
