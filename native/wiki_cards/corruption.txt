{{C|Corruption}} is a 3 (2 if upgraded) {{Icon|RE}}-cost {{QueryLink|Cards|color:Red|Ironclad}} {{QueryLink|Cards|rarity:Rare|Rare}} {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Power|Power}} Card. It gives 1 {{BD|Corruption}}, which makes all {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Skill|Skills}} have an {{Icon|RE}}-cost of 0, and {{KW|Exhaust|Exhausts}} them after they are played. As {{BD|Corruption}} does not stack, copies of {{C|Corruption}} played after the first have no additional effect.

== Interactions ==
=== Synergies ===
* Synergizes well with anything that benefits from {{KW|Exhaust}}, most notably {{C|Dark Embrace}}, as it allows you to keep drawing new {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Skill|Skills}}, and {{R|Dead Branch}}, as it allows you to keep playing any {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Skill|Skills}} you draw or create. 

=== Neutral ===
* With {{BD|Corruption}}, when randomizing the '''Energy''' {{Icon|RE}}-cost of cards through {{BD|Confused}} or {{P|Snecko Oil}}, the cost of all {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Skill|Skill}} cards will remain 0.
