{{C|Wild Strike}} is a 1 {{Icon|RE}}-cost {{QueryLink|Cards|color:Red|Ironclad}} {{QueryLink|Cards|rarity:Common|Common}} {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Attack|Attack}} Card. It deals 12 (17 when upgraded) damage and adds a {{C|Wound}} into your draw pile.

== Interactions ==
=== Order === 
* The {{C|Wound}} gets added into your draw pile ''before'' triggering {{R|Ink Bottle}}.

=== Synergies ===
* Synergizes with {{C|Fire Breathing}} and {{C|Evolve}}, as it creates a {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Status|Status}} card in your draw pile.
* {{C|Wild Strike}} gets a damage increase of 3 from {{R|Strike Dummy}}, and adds to the damage of {{C|Perfected Strike}}.
