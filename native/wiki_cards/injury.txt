{{C|Injury}} is an {{KW|Unplayable}} {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Curse|Curse}} Card.

== Obtaining Writhe ==
Like most {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Curse|Curses}}, Injury can be obtained from anything that gives a random Curse:
* Can be part of Neow's [[Third Blessing]] in exchange for a reward.
* {{E|Match and Keep}}.
* {{R|Cursed Key}}.
* {{KW|Transform|Transforming}} another Curse.

In addition, Injury can be obtained from the {{E|Golden Idol}} Event when choosing {{Frame|Take}} and then {{Frame|Outrun}}.

== Interactions ==
=== Synergies ===
As Injury is a '''{{QueryLink|Cards|type:Curse|Curse}}''', it interacts with the following:
* {{C|Fire Breathing}} will deal damage when it is drawn.
* {{R|Du-Vu Doll}} will give you 1 {{BD|Strength}} each combat.
* {{R|Blue Candle}} can be used to {{KW|Exhaust}} it for 1 HP. 
* When added to the deck, it will be negated by {{R|Omamori}}, or gain you 6 max HP if you have {{R|Darkstone Periapt}} and ''not'' Omamori.

=== Anti-Synergies ===
* As {{C|Clash}} can only be played when all cards in your hand are {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Attack|Attacks}}, if it is drawn at the same time as Injury, it cannot be played.

== Trivia ==
Injury can be seen as the Curse equivalent of the {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Status|Status}} {{C|Wound}}, with both being the most basic penalty cards of their respective types.
