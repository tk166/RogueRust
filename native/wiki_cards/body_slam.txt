{{C|Body Slam}} is a 1 (0 if upgraded) {{Icon|RE}}-cost {{QueryLink|Cards|color:Red|Ironclad}} {{QueryLink|Cards|rarity:Common|Common}} {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Attack|Attack}} Card. It deals damage equal to your current amount of {{KW|Block}}.

== Interactions ==
=== Order ===
* If playing this card triggers {{R|Ornamental Fan}}, it will deal damage before gaining the additional {{KW|Block}}, meaning it will not contribute to that damage.

=== Synergies ===
* Synergizes with ways to build up block, such as {{C|Barricade}} or {{C|Blur}} to maintain it and {{C|Entrench}} to scale it.
* If using {{R|Snecko Eye}} and upgraded with {{C|Armaments}} during combat, it's cost always turns to 0.

=== Neutral ===
* Regardless of dealing base damage equal to {{KW|Block}}, it still deals attack damage, and therefore also scales with {{BD|Strength}}.
