{{C|Bandage Up}} is a 0 {{Icon|CE}}-cost {{QueryLink|Cards|color:Colorless|Colorless}} {{QueryLink|Cards|rarity:Uncommon|Uncommon}} {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Skill|Skill}} Card. It heals 4 (6 if upgraded) HP, and then {{KW|Exhaust|Exhausts}}.

== Obtaining Bandage Up ==
{{C|Bandage Up}} is a {{QueryLink|Cards|color:Colorless|Colorless}} card, and therefore can be obtained in the following ways:
* Can be bought from the [[Merchant]].
* Can appear in the {{E|Sensory Stone}} event, from a choice of 3-9 {{QueryLink|Cards|color:Colorless|Colorless}} cards, depending on the option that is selected.
* Can be obtained from Neow's [[First Blessing]] via the {{TS|green||'''Obtain an Uncommon Colorless Card'''}} bonus.
* Can be obtained from [[Monsters]] and [[Elites]] with {{R|Prismatic Shard}}.
** Also found in the [[Custom Mode|Daily Climb]] with the {{KW|Diverse}} or {{KW|Colorless Cards}} modifiers.
* Can be obtained from the {{Frame|Success?}} option at {{E|Knowing Skull}}.

== Interactions ==
=== Synergies ===
* {{R|Magic Flower}} increases the effectiveness of the healing.

=== Neutral ===
* Healing cards cannot be generated mid-combat to avoid infinite healing, so {{C|Bandage Up}} cannot be created by {{C|Distraction}}, {{C|Chrysalis}} or {{R|Dead Branch}}.
