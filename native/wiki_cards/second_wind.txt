{{C|Second Wind}} is a 1 {{Icon|RE}}-cost {{QueryLink|Cards|color:Red|Ironclad}} {{QueryLink|Cards|rarity:Uncommon|Uncommon}} {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Skill|Skill}} Card. It {{KW|Exhaust|Exhausts}} all non-{{QueryLink|Cards|type:Attack|Attack}} cards in your hand, and you gain 5 (7 if upgraded) {{KW|Block}} for every card {{KW|Exhaust|Exhausted}} this way.

== Interactions ==
=== Order ===
* {{C|Second Wind}} will already be removed from hand when {{KW|Exhaust|Exhausting}} all non-{{QueryLink|Cards|type:Attack|Attack}} cards in your hand, meaning it will not {{KW|Exhaust}} itself.

=== Synergies ===
* {{C|Second Wind}} has {{KW|Exhaust}}-synergy with {{C|Dark Embrace}}, {{C|Feel No Pain}}, {{R|Charon's Ashes}} and {{R|Dead Branch}}, and can reliably {{KW|Exhaust}} {{C|Sentinel}}.
* Second Wind can be used to get rid of unwanted cards in hand, such as {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Status|Status}} or {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Curse|Curse}} cards.
* Each card {{KW|Exhaust|Exhausted}} counts as a separate instance of gaining {{KW|Block}}, meaning it scales well with {{BD|Dexterity}} and can trigger {{C|Juggernaut}} multiple times.
