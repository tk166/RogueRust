{{C|Feel No Pain}} is a 1 {{Icon|RE}}-cost {{QueryLink|Cards|color:Red|Ironclad}} {{QueryLink|Cards|rarity:Uncommon|Uncommon}} {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Power|Power}} Card. It gives 3 (4 if upgraded) {{BD|Feel No Pain}}, which gives that amount of {{KW|Block}} whenever a card is {{KW|Exhaust|Exhausted}}.

== Interactions ==
=== Order ===
* When playing a card that {{KW|Exhaust|Exhausts}} a card, {{BD|Feel No Pain}} gives {{KW|Block}} ''after'' the played card is resolved. 
** Enemy {{BD|Thorns}} deals knockback damage ''before'' {{BD|Feel No Pain}} gives {{KW|Block}} when playing an {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Attack|Attack}} card that {{KW|Exhaust|Exhausts}} something, such as {{C|Pummel}}, {{C|Sever Soul}} or {{C|Fiend Fire}}.
** {{BD|Beat of Death}} deals damage ''before'' {{BD|Feel No Pain}} gives {{KW|Block}} when {{KW|Exhaust|Exhausting}} another card because of a played card.

=== Neutral ===
* The {{KW|Block}} gained by {{BD|Feel No Pain}} is unaffected by {{BD|Frail}}, {{BD|Dexterity}} or {{BD|No Block}} from a previously played {{C|Panic Button}}.
