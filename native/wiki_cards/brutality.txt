{{C|Brutality}} is a 0 {{Icon|RE}}-cost {{QueryLink|Cards|color:Red|Ironclad}} {{QueryLink|Cards|rarity:Rare|Rare}} {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Power|Power}} Card. It gives 1 {{BD|Brutality}}, which costs that amount of HP and draws that amount of cards at the start of every turn.

== Interactions ==
=== Synergies ===
* {{R|Tungsten Rod}} completely negates the damage from the first stack of {{BD|Brutality}}.
* {{C|Brutality}} can be used to reliably and repeatedly trigger {{C|Rupture}}, {{R|Runic Cube}} and {{R|Self-Forming Clay}}, and lower the cost of {{C|Blood for Blood}}.

=== Neutral ===
* {{BD|Brutality}} can trigger {{R|Centennial Puzzle}}.
