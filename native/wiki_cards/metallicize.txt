{{C|Metallicize}} is a 1 {{Icon|RE}}-cost {{QueryLink|Cards|color:Red|Ironclad}} {{QueryLink|Cards|rarity:Uncommon|Uncommon}} {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Power|Power}} Card. It gives 3 (4 if upgraded) {{BD|Metallicize}}, which grants that amount of {{KW|Block}} at the end of every turn.

== Interactions ==
=== Order ===
* {{BD|Metallicize}} gives {{KW|Block}} ''after'' {{R|Orichalcum}} checks if you have block, meaning {{BD|Metallicize}} does not prevent the relic from activating.

=== Neutral ===
* The {{KW|Block}} gained by {{BD|Metallicize}} is unaffected by {{BD|Frail}}, {{BD|Dexterity}} or {{BD|No Block}} from a previously played {{C|Panic Button}}.
