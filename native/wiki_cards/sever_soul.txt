{{C|Sever Soul}} is a 2 {{Icon|RE}}-cost {{QueryLink|Cards|color:Red|Ironclad}} {{QueryLink|Cards|rarity:Uncommon|Uncommon}} {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Attack|Attack}} Card. It deals 16 (22 if upgraded) damage, and {{KW|Exhaust|Exhausts}} all non-{{QueryLink|Cards|type:Attack|Attack}} cards in your hand. 

== Interactions ==
=== Synergies ===
* {{C|Sever Soul}} has {{KW|Exhaust}}-synergy with {{C|Dark Embrace}}, {{C|Feel No Pain}}, {{R|Charon's Ashes}} and {{R|Dead Branch}}, and can reliably {{KW|Exhaust}} {{C|Sentinel}}.
* Sever Soul can be used to get rid of unwanted cards in hand, such as {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Status|Status}} or {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Curse|Curse}} cards.

=== Neutral ===
* The amount of cards {{KW|Exhaust|Exhausted}} through {{C|Sever Soul}} has no effect on the effectiveness of the card, even if no cards get {{KW|Exhaust|Exhausted}} at all.
