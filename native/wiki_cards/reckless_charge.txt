{{C|Reckless Charge}} is a 0 {{Icon|RE}}-cost {{QueryLink|Cards|color:Red|Ironclad}} {{QueryLink|Cards|rarity:Uncommon|Uncommon}} {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Attack|Attack}} Card. It deals 7 (10 if upgraded) damage and adds a {{C|Dazed}} into your draw pile.

== Interactions ==
=== Order === 
* The {{C|Dazed}} gets added into your draw pile ''before'' triggering {{R|Ink Bottle}}.

=== Synergies ===
* Synergizes with {{BD|Fire Breathing}} and {{BD|Evolve}}, as it creates a {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Status|Status}} card in your draw pile.

* The {{C|Dazed}} generated by this can be used as a part of {{KW|Exhaust}} synergy, especially in combination with {{R|Medical Kit}}.
