{{C|Clash}} is a 0 {{Icon|RE}}-cost {{QueryLink|Cards|color:Red|Ironclad}} {{QueryLink|Cards|rarity:Common|Common}} {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Attack|Attack}} Card. It deals 14 (18 if upgraded) damage. It cannot be played if any card in hand is not an {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Attack|Attack}} Card.

== Interactions ==
=== Synergies ===
* {{C|Sever Soul}} and {{C|Second Wind}}, and to a lesser degree {{C|Corruption}}, {{R|Blue Candle}}, and {{R|Medical Kit}} can be used to fulfill the requirement to play this card. 
* Synergizes well with hand-management through effects such as {{KW|Exhaust}} or [[Keywords#Discard|Discard]].
* When drawing {{C|Clash}} through {{R|Unceasing Top}}, it will be the only card in hand, meaning it can be played.

=== Neutral ===
* If the condition is not met, {{C|Clash}} will simply not be able to be played. This includes if it is played indirectly through effects such as {{P|Distilled Chaos}} or {{C|Havoc}}. These will cause {{C|Clash}} to simply discard itself without being played, unless {{KW|Exhaust|Exhausted}}. This means:
** [[Time Eater]]'s {{BD|Time Warp}} is not triggered.
** {{R|Ink Bottle}} is not advanced.
** {{R|Nunchaku}}, {{R|Kunai}}, {{R|Shuriken}} and {{R|Ornamental Fan}} are not triggered.

* While {{C|Havoc}} will not play an unplayable {{C|Clash}}, it will still {{KW|Exhaust}} it. This means all {{KW|Exhaust}} synergies will still trigger, like {{C|Feel No Pain}}.

=== Anti-Synergies ===
* As opposed to most cards and strategies in this game, this card pairs poorly with effects that allow you to draw cards, except for previously mentioned {{R|Unceasing Top}}. 
* It pairs poorly with {{C|Dark Embrace}} or {{R|Dead Branch}}, since {{KW|Exhaust|Exhausting}} non-{{QueryLink|Cards|type:Attack|Attack}} cards is a way to enable you to play {{C|Clash}}, but either of these can possibly replace those cards with other non-{{QueryLink|Cards|type:Attack|Attack}} cards.
