{{C|Rampage}} is a 1 {{Icon|RE}}-cost {{QueryLink|Cards|color:Red|Ironclad}} {{QueryLink|Cards|rarity:Uncommon|Uncommon}} {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Attack|Attack}} Card. It deals 8 damage, and increases this damage by 5 (8 if upgraded) for the rest of combat.

== Interactions ==
=== Synergies ===
* It synergizes with anything that allows it to be played sooner, such as {{C|Headbutt}}, {{P|Liquid Memories}} or {{C|Secret Weapon}}.
** Decreasing the size of the deck through {{KW|Exhaust|Exhausting}} cards can also increase the speed at which {{C|Rampage}} is played.

* Playing it twice using an effect such as {{C|Double Tap}} or a {{P|Duplication Potion}} will scale it twice as well, and the second play already has a damage increase from the first play.

=== Neutral ===
* Using {{C|Dual Wield}} or {{C|Nightmare}} for creating copies of {{C|Rampage}} during combat will copy the current damage, but afterwards these copies will scale differently.

* Adding more copies of {{C|Rampage}} to the deck does not increase scaling speed, as each copy scales separately.

* Only the actual damage is affected by {{BD|Strength}}, the damage with which the card scales is not.
