{{C|Apparition}} is a 1 {{Icon|CE}}-cost {{QueryLink|Cards|color:Colorless|Colorless}} {{QueryLink|Cards|rarity:Special|Special}} {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Skill|Skill}} Card. It gives 1 {{BD|Intangible}} and then {{KW|Exhaust|Exhausts}}. Unless upgraded, it has {{KW|Ethereal}}, which means it will {{KW|Exhaust}} if it is in your hand when you end your turn.

== Obtaining Apparition ==
{{C|Apparition}} is an [[Events]]-only card, and the only way to obtain it is through the event {{E|Council of Ghosts}}, which will give 5 copies of {{C|Apparition}}, or 3 copies of {{C|Apparition}} above Ascension {{Asc|15}}.
