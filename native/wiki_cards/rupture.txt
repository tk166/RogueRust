{{C|Rupture}} is a 1 {{Icon|RE}}-cost {{QueryLink|Cards|color:Red|Ironclad}} {{QueryLink|Cards|rarity:Uncommon|Uncommon}} {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Power|Power}} Card. It gives 1 (2 if upgraded) {{BD|Rupture}}, which gives that amount of {{BD|Strength}} whenever a card causes you to lose HP.

== Interactions ==
=== Order ===
* {{C|Hemokinesis}} will not benefit from the {{BD|Strength}} gained as a result of {{BD|Rupture}} until played again, as {{BD|Rupture}} triggers ''after'' the card is already resolved.

* HP loss caused by {{C|Pain}} applies ''after'' the effects of the card that triggered it, meaning {{BD|Rupture}} will trigger ''after'' the card that triggered {{C|Pain}} has already dealt damage.
** However, if you have {{C|Pain}} in hand and use a different card to cause an {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Attack|Attack}} to be played, such as through {{C|Havoc}}, the first card will trigger {{C|Pain}} and therefore {{BD|Rupture}} ''before'' the {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Attack|Attack}} takes effect.

=== Synergies ===
* Playing any {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Curse|Curse}} with {{R|Blue Candle}} triggers {{BD|Rupture}}.

* {{C|Reaper}} gives healing based on damage dealt, and therefore increases with {{BD|Strength}}. This can be used to regain HP lost as a result of triggering this card.

* Any card that can cause you to lose HP can trigger {{BD|Rupture}}:
==== Cards ====
* {{C|Bloodletting}}, {{C|Hemokinesis}} and {{C|J.A.X.}} can repeatedly be played to trigger it.
* {{C|Offering}} can be played to trigger it and then {{KW|Exhaust|Exhausts}}.
* {{C|Combust}} and {{C|Brutality}} will trigger it every turn.
* {{C|Regret}} will trigger it if it is in your hand at the end of your turn.
* {{C|Pain}} makes every other card in hand cost 1 HP, allowing you to rack up large quantities of {{BD|Strength}} quickly.
* {{C|Burn}} and {{C|Decay}} deal damage instead of causing HP loss directly. This will still trigger {{BD|Rupture}}, as long as the damage is not fully negated by {{KW|Block}}, and therefore causes you to lose HP.

=== Neutral ===
* The amount of {{BD|Strength}} gained from triggering {{BD|Rupture}} does not scale with the amount of HP lost.

* Losing HP when playing a card, as a result of {{BD|Thorns}}, {{BD|Sharp Hide}} or {{BD|Beat of Death}}, will not trigger {{BD|Rupture}}.

== Trivia ==
* Technically {{C|Blasphemy}} deals 99999 damage instead of killing the player directly. In theory this could be used to activate {{C|Rupture}}, although unpractical in reality.
