{{C|Double Tap}} is a 1 {{Icon|RE}} cost {{QueryLink|Cards|color:Red|Ironclad}} {{QueryLink|Cards|rarity:Rare|Rare}} {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Skill|Skill}} Card. It gives 1 (2 if upgraded) stacks of {{BD|Double Tap}}, which will make the following {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Attack|Attack}} card(s) this turn play twice, for the first amount of cards equal to the amount of {{BD|Double Tap}}. 

Having more than 1 {{BD|Double Tap}} will not increase the amount of times a single {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Attack|Attack}} card is duplicated, but increases the amount of cards that can be duplicated when played.

The second play of these cards counts as a separate card being played, but this card is not added to your draw or discard pile, and is not {{KW|Exhaust|Exhausted}} unless the card would {{KW|Exhaust}} upon being played normally. 

The second play of these cards does not expend any {{Icon|RE}} '''Energy'''. The second play of an X-cost {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Attack|Attack}} card will use the same value of X as the first.

== Interactions ==
=== Order ===
* The second play of an {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Attack|Attack}} card only starts after the first play is fully resolved. In effect, it is as if the two cards were played with a pause in between, resulting in interactions including but not limited to:
** The second play of {{C|Headbutt}} will be able to target the first play in the discard pile. 
** {{BD|Vigor}} will be consumed by the first play, and will not be used for the second play.
** {{BD|Strength}} gained by {{R|Shuriken}} by the first play will already affect the second play.
** {{BD|Malleable}} will already give the target {{KW|Block}} after the first play.

=== Neutral ===
* The second play of an {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Attack|Attack}} card counts as the play of a wholly separate card. All consequences of playing that card will still apply:
** The effects of the card itself.
** Anything that triggers or increments upon playing an ({{QueryLink|Cards|type:Attack|Attack}}) card, including but not limited to: 
*** Effects such as {{BD|Rage}}.
*** Relics such as {{R|Shuriken}} and {{R|Ink Bottle}}.
*** Card limits such as {{R|Velvet Choker}} and the Time Eater's {{BD|Time Warp}}.  

== Trivia ==

* Along with {{C|Burst}} and {{C|Amplify}}, one of the three 'play a card twice' Skills in the game.
