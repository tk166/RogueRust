{{C|Blood for Blood}} is a 4 (3 if upgraded) {{Icon|RE}}-cost {{QueryLink|Cards|color:Red|Ironclad}} {{QueryLink|Cards|rarity:Uncommon|Uncommon}} {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Attack|Attack}} Card. It deals 18 (22 if upgraded) damage, and costs 1 less {{Icon|RE}} '''Energy''' for every time you have lost HP this combat, no matter the source or amount of this HP loss.

== Interactions ==
=== Synergies ===
* Unlike most other cards with {{Icon|RE}} '''Energy'''-reducing upgrades that have had their cost randomized by {{BD|Confused}} or {{P|Snecko Oil}}, a {{C|Blood for Blood}} that costs more than 0 {{Icon|RE}} '''Energy''' will always go down by 1 in {{Icon|RE}}-cost when {{KW|Upgrade|Upgraded}}.

=== Neutral ===
* When reducing the {{Icon|RE}}-cost of {{C|Blood for Blood}} through an effect such as {{P|Liquid Memories}} or {{C|Enlightenment}}, losing HP or upgrading {{C|Blood for Blood}} will continue to lower the cost as normal.
** If the original {{Icon|RE}}-cost reduction was temporary, such as through an unupgraded {{C|Enlightenment}}, any cost reductions as a result of upgrading or losing HP will apply to the cost {{C|Blood for Blood}} had before this reduction once it wears off.

* If this card is created during combat, such as through {{C|Metamorphosis}}, an {{P|Attack Potion}} or {{R|Dead Branch}}, it will account for HP lost before it existed. 
** This is unlike {{C|Masterful Stab}}, which will start at 0 {{Icon|GE}}-cost. 

== Trivia ==
* '''Blood For Blood''' was briefly included in Slay the Spire 2's early development, before being removed in [[sts2:V0.40.0 - Pre-release Version|V0.40.0]] along with Entrench.<ref>https://youtu.be/ttVtllHkb4E&t=50</ref>
