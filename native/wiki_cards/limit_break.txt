{{C|Limit Break}} is a 1 {{Icon|RE}}-cost {{QueryLink|Cards|color:Red|Ironclad}} {{QueryLink|Cards|rarity:Rare|Rare}} {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Skill|Skill}} Card. It doubles the amount of {{BD|Strength}} you have. {{C|Limit Break}} will then {{KW|Exhaust}} itself, if it is not upgraded.
