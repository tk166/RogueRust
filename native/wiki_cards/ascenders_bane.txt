{{C|Ascender's Bane}} is an {{KW|Unplayable}} {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Curse|Curse}} Card. It is {{KW|Ethereal}}, and cannot be '''removed''' from your deck or {{KW|Transform|Transformed}} by any means.

== Obtaining Ascender's Bane ==
On {{Asc|10|Ascension 10}} and higher, runs begin with a copy of '''Ascender's Bane''' in your starting deck. As there is no way to remove the card, high Ascension runs must always contend with at least one {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Curse|Curse}} in the deck.

As there is no other way to obtain a copy, '''Ascender's Bane''' is the only piece of game content locked behind [[Ascension]].

== Interactions ==
=== Order ===
{{KW|Ethereal}} causes '''Ascender's Bane''' to {{KW|Exhaust}} after your hand is discarded for the turn. If this causes you to draw from {{C|Dark Embrace}} or gain a card from {{R|Dead Branch}}, those cards will remain in your hand until your next turn.

=== Synergies ===
As '''Ascender's Bane''' is a '''{{QueryLink|Cards|type:Curse|Curse}}''', it interacts with the following:
* {{C|Fire Breathing}} will deal '''6(10)''' damage when it is drawn.
* {{R|Du-Vu Doll}} will give you '''+1''' {{BD|Strength}} each combat.
* {{R|Blue Candle}} can be used to {{KW|Exhaust}} it for 1 HP. 
** However, as '''Ascender's Bane''' generally '''Exhausts''' at end of turn anyway, this is only helpful in unusual circumstances. For instance, losing the '''HP''' can trigger {{R|Centennial Puzzle}} at will.
* When added to the deck, it will be negated by {{R|Omamori}}, or gain you '''6 max HP''' if you have {{R|Darkstone Periapt}} and ''not'' Omamori. However, since you start the game with your original copy, the only way for this to happen is if you duplicate the original.
As Ascender's Bane is {{KW|Ethereal}}, it interacts with the following:
* When it '''Exhausts''', {{C|Feel No Pain}} gains you {{KW|Block}}.
* Similarly, {{C|Dark Embrace}} will draw you cards, and {{R|Dead Branch}} will generate a card to replace it. These cards will be kept in your hand until your next turn.
* {{R|Charon's Ashes}} will deal damage to all enemies.
* {{R|Strange Spoon}} only affects cards that {{KW|Exhaust}} when played, so it will never prevent '''Ascender's Bane''' from '''Exhausting''' (unless you are doing so with {{R|Blue Candle}}).

=== Neutral ===
* Being {{KW|Ethereal}}, Ascender's Bane will {{KW|Exhaust}} if it is in your hand at end of turn, even if it is {{KW|Retain|Retained}}.

=== Anti-Synergies ===
* If '''Ascender's Bane''' is discarded, you may end up drawing it again. This is sometimes the correct thing to do, but it does introduce a downside to {{C|Calculated Gamble}}, {{C|Storm of Steel}}, and {{C|Unload}}, which will all force you to discard it.
** {{C|All-Out Attack}} may also sometimes discard it unnecessarily.
* As {{C|Clash}} can only be played when all cards in your hand are {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Attack|Attacks}}, if it is drawn at the same time as '''Ascender's Bane''', it cannot be played.

== Trivia ==
Despite '''Ascender's Bane''''s in game text only saying it cannot be '''removed''', it also can't be '''Transformed'''. However it can be '''duplicated''' using {{E|Duplicator}} and {{R|Dolly's Mirror}}.
