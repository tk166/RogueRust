{{C|Havoc}} is a 1 (0 if upgraded) {{Icon|RE}}-cost {{QueryLink|Cards|color:Red|Ironclad}} {{QueryLink|Cards|rarity:Common|Common}} {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Attack|Attack}} Card. It plays and {{KW|Exhaust|Exhausts}} the top card of your draw pile, without expending any {{Icon|RE}} '''Energy'''. X-cost cards will use your current {{Icon|RE}} '''Energy''' for X, without consuming that {{Icon|RE}} '''Energy'''.

== Interactions ==
=== Order ===
* The top card being played and being {{KW|Exhaust|Exhausted}} are separate, meaning the resolution of the played card takes place ''before'' any results of {{KW|Exhaust|Exhausting}} that card can occur.
** This means that {{BD|Beat of Death}} will trigger ''before'' {{C|Feel No Pain}} gives {{KW|Block}} as a result of the card being {{KW|Exhaust|Exhausted}} by {{C|Havoc}}.

=== Synergies ===
* {{C|Headbutt}}, {{C|Warcry}} and {{C|Thinking Ahead}} let you manipulate the top card of your draw pile, giving you more control over {{C|Havoc}}.
* {{R|Frozen Eye}} allows you to see which card {{C|Havoc}} will target.

=== Neutral ===
* If your draw pile is empty when playing {{C|Havoc}}, it will shuffle your discard pile into your draw pile and target the new top card.
* {{R|Strange Spoon}} has a 50% chance of saving a card that would be {{KW|Exhaust|Exhausted}} by {{C|Havoc}}.
