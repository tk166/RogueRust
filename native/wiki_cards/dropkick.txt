{{C|Dropkick}} is a 1 {{Icon|RE}}-cost {{QueryLink|Cards|color:Red|Ironclad}} {{QueryLink|Cards|rarity:Uncommon|Uncommon}} {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Attack|Attack}} Card. It deals 5 (8 if upgraded) damage. If the target has {{BD|Vulnerable}}, gain 1 {{Icon|RE}} '''Energy''' and draw 1 card.

== Interactions ==
=== Neutral ===
* No matter what {{Icon|RE}}-cost {{C|Dropkick}} has, the player will always gain 1 {{Icon|RE}} '''Energy''' if it is played on a target with {{BD|Vulnerable}}. This can be used to your advantage by reducing the cost below 1, but can lose you {{Icon|RE}} '''Energy''' as well if the cost is increased:

==== Cards ====
* {{C|Setup}} sets the cost to 0, resulting in 1 {{Icon|RE}} '''Energy''' gain.
* {{C|Bullet Time}} sets the cost to 0, resulting in 1 {{Icon|RE}} '''Energy''' gain, although this extra energy is often useless.
* {{C|Madness}} sets the cost to 0, resulting in 1 {{Icon|RE}} '''Energy''' gain.

==== Relics and potions ====
* If {{C|Dropkick}} is selected from an {{P|Attack Potion}} it will have a cost of 0, resulting in 1 {{Icon|RE}} '''Energy''' gain.
* {{BD|Confused}} and {{P|Snecko Oil}} will randomize the cost from 0 to 3, which can result in a neutral, gained or lost amount of energy.
