{{C|Feed}} is a 1 {{Icon|RE}}-cost {{QueryLink|Cards|color:Red|Ironclad}} {{QueryLink|Cards|rarity:Rare|Rare}} {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Attack|Attack}} Card. It deals 10 (12 if upgraded) damage, and if this damage is {{KW|Fatal}}, it will permanently increase your Max HP by 3 (4 if upgraded). {{C|Feed}} will then {{KW|Exhaust}} itself.

== Interactions ==
=== Synergies ===
* As playing {{C|Feed}} at the right moment is crucial, it synergizes with {{R|Runic Pyramid}} and ways to {{KW|Retain}} it, such as {{C|Well-Laid Plans}}, {{C|Equilibrium}} or {{C|Meditate}}.

* As one of the few cards that has benefits that remain outside of combat, triggering it as often as possible becomes an additional objective within combat. Given that {{C|Feed}} {{KW|Exhaust|Exhausts}} after use, it synergizes with:
** {{R|Strange Spoon}}, which gives a 50% chance to not {{KW|Exhaust}} {{C|Feed}} when played, allowing it to be redrawn and used again.
** {{C|Exhume}}, which can get {{C|Feed}} back once after being played, with the added benefit of putting it directly into your hand.

=== Neutral ===
* When duplicated through an effect such as {{C|Double Tap}} or a {{P|Duplication Potion}}, {{C|Feed}} will trigger its {{KW|Fatal}} effect even if only the second play was {{KW|Fatal}}.
** As duplicated {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Attack|Attacks}} always target the same enemy with both plays, you cannot trigger {{C|Feed}} twice by duplicating it.

* {{KW|Fatal}} does ''not'' trigger when {{BD|Minion|Minions}} are killed.
** However, the {{M|Cultist}} enemies in {{M|Awakened One}} fight are not technically {{BD|Minion|Minions}}, and ''can'' trigger {{KW|Fatal}}.

* {{KW|Fatal}} is not affected by {{M|Slime Boss}} or other Slime enemies splitting, and only triggers when reducing a Slime's health to 0, whether that Slime was spawned as the result of a split or not.
* The first phase of {{M|Awakened One}} does not trigger {{KW|Fatal}}, but the second phase does.

* In the {{M|Darkling|Three Darklings}} fight, an attack is only considered {{KW|Fatal}} if it kills the last {{M|Darkling}}; otherwise, unlimited triggers could be farmed on this fight.

* {{KW|Fatal}} triggers on killing the {{M|Corrupt Heart}}, although this does not matter, as it ends the run.

=== Anti-Synergies ===
* Due to the limited number of {{KW|Fatal}} triggers available in any given combat, {{C|Feed}} does not synergize with other {{KW|Fatal}} cards:
** {{C|Hand of Greed}}
** {{C|Ritual Dagger}}
** {{C|Lesson Learned}}
* {{R|Mark of the Bloom}} prevents the healing from the Max HP increase, however the player will still gain Max HP.

== Achievements ==
=== Ooh Donut! ===
* Unlocks when you kill {{M|Donu}} with '''Feed'''.
