{{C|Barricade}} is a 3 (2 if upgraded) {{Icon|RE}}-cost {{QueryLink|Cards|color:Red|Ironclad}} {{QueryLink|Cards|rarity:Rare|Rare}} {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Power|Power}} Card. It gives 1 {{BD|Barricade}}, which prevents your {{KW|Block}} from being removed in between turns. As {{BD|Barricade}} does not stack, copies of {{C|Barricade}} played after the first have no additional effect.

== Interactions ==
=== Order ===
* {{BD|Barricade}} takes precedence over {{R|Calipers}}, effectively removing all effects of the relic.

=== Synergies ===
* Synergizes well with ways to build up block, most notably {{C|Entrench}}.
* Can enable {{C|Body Slam}} to do more damage through building up {{KW|Block}} over multiple turns.
