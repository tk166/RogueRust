{{C|True Grit}} is a 1 {{Icon|RE}}-cost {{QueryLink|Cards|color:Red|Ironclad}} {{QueryLink|Cards|rarity:Common|Common}} {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Skill|Skill}} Card. It gives 7 {{KW|Block}} and randomly {{KW|Exhaust|Exhausts}} one card in your hand. If it is upgraded, it allows you to select one card in hand to {{KW|Exhaust}} instead. If there are no other cards in hand when this card is played, no card will be {{KW|Exhaust|Exhausted}}.

== Interactions ==
=== Synergies ===
* {{C|True Grit}} has {{KW|Exhaust}}-synergy with {{C|Dark Embrace}}, {{C|Feel No Pain}}, {{R|Charon's Ashes}}, and {{R|Dead Branch}}, and, if upgraded, can reliably {{KW|Exhaust}} {{C|Sentinel}}.
* Upgraded {{C|True Grit}} can be used to get rid of unwanted cards in hand, such as {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Status|Status}} or {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Curse|Curse}} cards.
