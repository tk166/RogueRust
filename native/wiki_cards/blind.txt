{{C|Blind}} is a 0 {{Icon|CE}}-cost {{QueryLink|Cards|color:Colorless|Colorless}} {{QueryLink|Cards|rarity:Uncommon|Uncommon}} {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Skill|Skill}} Card. It applies 2 {{BD|Weak}} to the target, or to '''ALL''' enemies if upgraded.

== Obtaining Blind ==
{{C|Blind}} is a {{QueryLink|Cards|color:Colorless|Colorless}} card, and therefore can be obtained in the following ways:
* Can be bought from the [[Merchant]].
* Can appear in the {{E|Sensory Stone}} event, from a choice of 3-9 {{QueryLink|Cards|color:Colorless|Colorless}} cards, depending on the option that is selected.
* Can be obtained from Neow's [[First Blessing]] via the {{TS|green||'''Obtain an Uncommon Colorless Card'''}} bonus.
* Can be obtained from [[Monsters]] and [[Elites]] with {{R|Prismatic Shard}}.
** Also found in [[Custom Mode|Daily Climb]] with the {{KW|Diverse}} or {{KW|Colorless Cards}} modifiers.
* Can be obtained from the {{Frame|Success?}} option at {{E|Knowing Skull}}.
