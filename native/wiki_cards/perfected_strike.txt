{{C|Perfected Strike}} is a 2 {{Icon|RE}}-cost {{QueryLink|Cards|color:Red|Ironclad}} {{QueryLink|Cards|rarity:Common|Common}} {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Attack|Attack}} Card. It deals 6 damage, which is increased by 2 (3 if upgraded) for every card with "Strike" in its name that is in your deck when this card is played.

== Interactions ==
=== Synergies ===
* {{R|Strike Dummy}} increases the damage of this card by 3.

* Any card with "Strike" in the name increases the damage of this card by 2 (3 if this {{C|Perfected Strike}} upgraded):
==== Cards ====
* All basic Strikes: {{C|Strike (Ironclad)|Strike}}, {{C|Strike (Silent)|Strike}}, {{C|Strike (Defect)|Strike}}, and {{C|Strike (Watcher)|Strike}}.
* {{C|Perfected Strike}} increases not only the damage of other copies, but also of itself.
* {{C|Pommel Strike}}, {{C|Twin Strike}}, {{C|Wild Strike}}.
* {{C|Swift Strike}}.
* {{C|Sneaky Strike}}, {{C|Thunder Strike}}, {{C|Meteor Strike}}, {{C|Windmill Strike}}.

=== Neutral ===
* The damage is calculated at the time of playing {{C|Perfected Strike}}. This means that getting rid of cards with "Strike" in their name during combat, such as by {{KW|Exhaust|Exhausting}}, will decrease the damage dealt by {{C|Perfected Strike}}. Similarly, increasing the amount of those cards through for example {{R|Dead Branch}} or {{C|Dual Wield}} will increase the damage.

== Trivia ==
* This card's [[Beta Art]] shows a {{M|Gremlin Wizard}} admiring the [[Ironclad]]'s new sword.
