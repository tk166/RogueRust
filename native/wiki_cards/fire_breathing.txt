== Interactions ==
=== Neutral ===
* {{BD|Fire Breathing}} only activates upon {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Status|Status}} or {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Curse|Curse}} cards being drawn. This means that cards created in hand, such as through {{C|Power Through}}, do not trigger it.
