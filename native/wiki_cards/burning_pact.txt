{{C|Burning Pact}} is a 1 {{Icon|RE}}-cost {{QueryLink|Cards|color:Red|Ironclad}} {{QueryLink|Cards|rarity:Uncommon|Uncommon}} {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Skill|Skill}} Card. It makes you select one card in hand to {{KW|Exhaust}} and draws 2 (3 if upgraded) cards.

== Interactions ==
=== Order ===
* Selecting a card to {{KW|Exhaust}} happens ''before'' drawing, meaning a card that is already in hand when this card is played must be {{KW|Exhaust|Exhausted}}. If there are no other cards in hand when this card is played, no card will be {{KW|Exhaust|Exhausted}}.

=== Synergies ===
* Has {{KW|Exhaust}}-synergy with {{C|Dark Embrace}}, {{C|Feel No Pain}}, {{R|Charon's Ashes}} and {{R|Dead Branch}}, and can reliably {{KW
|Exhaust}} {{C|Sentinel}}.
* {{C|Burning Pact}} can be used to get rid of unwanted cards in hand, such as {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Status|Status}} or {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Curse|Curse}} cards.
