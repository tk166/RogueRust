{{C|Wish}} is a 3 {{Icon|PE}}-cost {{QueryLink|Cards|color:Purple|Watcher}} {{QueryLink|Cards|rarity:Rare|Rare}} {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Skill|Skill}} Card. It gives the choice between 3 cards, of which the chosen one gets played immediately:
* {{C|Live Forever}}, which gives 6 (8 if {{C|Wish}} was upgraded) {{BD|Plated Armor}}
* {{C|Become Almighty}}, which gives 3 (4 if {{C|Wish}} was upgraded) {{BD|Strength}}
* {{C|Fame and Fortune}}, which gives 25 (30 if {{C|Wish}} was upgraded) [[Gold]].

== Interactions ==
=== Neutral ===
* Despite the fact that {{C|Wish}} technically creates a card, {{C|Master Reality}} does NOT upgrade that card. Upgrading {{C|Wish}} itself is the only way to upgrade the cards that it creates.

== Trivia ==
* {{C|Wish}} is the only card in the game that creates, then immediately plays a card. The three cards that {{C|Wish}} creates never enter your hand and cannot be added to the deck.
* The beta design of '''Become Almighty''' is a reference to All Might, a character from My Hero Academia.
