{{C|Pommel Strike}} is a 1 {{Icon|RE}}-cost {{QueryLink|Cards|color:Red|Ironclad}} {{QueryLink|Cards|rarity:Common|Common}} {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Attack|Attack}} Card. It deals 9 (10 if upgraded) damage, and draws 1 card (2 cards if upgraded).

== Interactions ==
=== Synergies ===
* {{C|Pommel Strike}} gets a damage increase of 3 from {{R|Strike Dummy}}, and adds to the damage of {{C|Perfected Strike}}.

* Two copies of upgraded Pommel Strike can be used to trigger {{R|Sundial}} perpetually. This combination can be further improved with something like {{R|The Abacus}} or {{R|Ornamental Fan}}.
