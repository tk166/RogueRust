{{C|Dazed}} is an {{KW|Unplayable}} {{QueryLink|Cards|color:Colorless|Colorless}} {{QueryLink|Cards|Common:Common|Common}} {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Status|Status}} Card.

== Obtaining Dazed ==
Playing {{C|Reckless Charge}} will add a {{C|Dazed}} to the player's draw pile.

The following enemies can give the player {{C|Dazed}}:
* {{M|Chosen}}
** {{Int|Chosen|Hex}} - Applies {{BD|Hex}}.
** {{BD|Hex}} - Whenever you play a non-{{QueryLink|Cards|type:Attack|Attack}} card, shuffle X {{C|Dazed}} into your draw pile.
* {{M|Repulsor}}
** {{Int|Repulsor|Daze}} - Adds 2 {{C|Dazed}} into your draw pile.
* {{M|Sentry}}
** {{Int|Sentry|Bolt}} - Adds 2({{Asc|18|3}}) {{C|Dazed}} into your discard pile.
* {{M|Deca}}
** {{Int|Deca|Beam}} - Adds 2 {{C|Dazed}} into your discard pile.
* {{M|Corrupt Heart}}
** {{Int|Corrupt Heart|Debilitate}} - Adds 1 {{C|Dazed}} into your draw pile.

== Interactions ==

=== Synergies ===
* Triggers the damage from {{C|Fire Breathing}}.
* Draws cards with {{C|Evolve}}.
* Is {{KW|Exhaust|Exhausted}} by {{C|Second Wind}} and triggers its {{KW|Block}} gain.
* Can be {{KW|Exhaust|Exhausted}} with {{R|Medical Kit}} to trigger exhaust synergies such as {{C|Feel No Pain}} or {{R|Charon's Ashes}}, or will {{KW|Exhaust}} on its own at the end of your turn.

=== Neutral ===
* Is {{KW|Exhaust|Exhausted}} by {{C|Sever Soul}} since it's not an {{QueryLink|Cards|Type:Attack|Attack}}.

=== Anti-Synergies ===
* Prevents {{C|Clash}} from being played.
