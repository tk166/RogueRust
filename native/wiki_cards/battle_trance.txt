{{C|Battle Trance}} is a 0 {{Icon|RE}}-cost {{QueryLink|Cards|color:Red|Ironclad}} {{QueryLink|Cards|rarity:Uncommon|Uncommon}} {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Skill|Skill}} Card. It draws 3 (4 if upgraded) cards and gives one {{BD|No Draw}}, which prevents cards from being drawn for the rest of your turn.

== Interactions ==
=== Synergies ===
* {{R|Orange Pellets}} can remove the {{BD|No Draw}} you get from this card.
* Sources of {{BD|Artifact}}, such as {{R|Clockwork Souvenir}}, {{P|Ancient Potion}}, {{C|Core Surge}} and {{C|Panacea}} can prevent this card from applying {{BD|No Draw}}.
