'''Gremlin Nob''' is an [[Elite]] found in [[Act 1]] and in the [[Act 2]] event {{E|The Colosseum}}.

Always starts with {{Int|Gremlin Nob|Bellow}} then has a 33% chance of using {{Int|Gremlin Nob|Skull Bash}} and a 67% chance of using {{Int|Gremlin Nob|Bull Rush}}. Cannot use {{Int|Gremlin Nob|Bull Rush}} three times in a row.

At {{Asc|18|Ascension}}, '''Gremlin Nob''' uses {{Int|Gremlin Nob|Skull Bash}} on the 2nd turn and uses {{Int|Gremlin Nob|Bull Rush}} twice afterwards, then repeats this pattern for the rest of the fight.

== Tips ==
The player is heavily encouraged to not play {{QueryLink|Cards|type:Skill|Skills}} after '''Turn 1''' of this fight, and to try to end this fight by '''Turn 3''', however, some cards like {{C|Glacier}} and {{C|Leg Sweep}} tend to still be very positive if a '''Turn 3''' kill is no longer possible.
