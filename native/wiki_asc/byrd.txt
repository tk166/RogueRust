Byrd never starts with {{Int|Byrd|Swoop}}. While it has {{BD|Flying}}, Byrd will use one of the following actions each turn:
* {{Int|Byrd|Peck}} (50% chance)
* {{Int|Byrd|Caw}} (30% chance)
* {{Int|Byrd|Swoop}} (20% chance)
Byrd cannot use {{Int|Byrd|Caw}} and {{Int|Byrd|Swoop}} twice in a row, or {{Int|Byrd|Peck}} three times in a row.

=== Pattern While Grounded [[File:ByrdGrounded.png|80px|frameless]] ===
If Byrd does not have {{BD|Flying}}, it will use the following actions in order before returning to its regular pattern:
#{{Int|Byrd|Stunned}} (replaces current [[Intent]] when {{BD|Flying}} is lost)
#{{Int|Byrd|Headbutt}}
#{{Int|Byrd|Go Airborne}}

== Tips ==
* Indirect damage bypasses {{BD|Flying}}. This includes damage from [[Orbs]], {{BD|Poison}}, and many other [[Debuffs]]/[[Buffs]].

* Using {{C|Vault}} does not reset {{BD|Flying}}'s counter.

== Trivia ==
* Byrds appear in the [[Card]] art for {{C|Chill}}.
