'''Orb Walker''' is a normal [[Monsters|Enemy]] encountered exclusively in [[Act 3]]. It can also be encountered in the {{E|Mysterious Sphere}} [[Event]].

'''Orb Walker''' will use one of the following actions each turn:
* {{Int|Orb Walker|Laser}} (60% chance)
* {{Int|Orb Walker|Claw}} (40% chance)
'''Orb Walker''' cannot use either action three times in a row.

== In Party With ==
=== Act 3 ===

* Solo

In the {{E|Mysterious Sphere}} [[Event]] alongside:
* 1 other '''Orb Walker'''

== Tips ==

* The Orb Walker scales strength somewhat quickly, with the added threat of diluting your draw pile with [[Burn|Burns]]. End the fight quickly and, if necessary, consider taking damage in early turns to play critical cards.
* {{BD|Weak}} is effective at blocking large amounts of damage if the fight is drawn out.
