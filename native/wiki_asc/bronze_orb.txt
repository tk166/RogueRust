Each '''Bronze Orb''' can only use {{Int|Bronze Orb|Stasis}} once per combat. {{Int|Bronze Orb|Stasis}} has a 75% chance to be used each turn until it has been picked. After using {{Int|Bronze Orb|Stasis}} '''Bronze Orb''' has a 70% chance to use {{Int|Bronze Orb|Support Beam}} and a 30% chance to use {{Int|Bronze Orb|Beam}} each turn. '''Bronze Orb''' cannot use the same attack thrice in a row.
=== Stasis ===
{{Int|Bronze Orb|Stasis}} will always prioritize the highest [[Cards#Card Rarity|rarity]] Card in your draw pile. If your draw pile is empty, {{Int|Bronze Orb|Stasis}} will pick from your discard pile instead. Cards in {{BD|Stasis}} will not be affected by certain variables; for example, {{C|Masterful Stab}}'s {{KW|Energy}} cost will not increase even if the player takes damage.
