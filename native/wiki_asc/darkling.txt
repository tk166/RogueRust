On turn one all Darklings have a 50/50 chance of using either {{Int|Darkling|Nip}} or {{Int|Darkling|Harden}}. The middle Darkling continues this pattern but cannot use {{Int|Darkling|Chomp}}.

'''The outer two Darklings on all other turns:'''
* {{Int|Darkling|Nip}} 30% chance
* {{Int|Darkling|Chomp}} 40% chance
* {{Int|Darkling|Harden}} 30% chance

Neither {{Int|Darkling|Harden}} nor {{Int|Darkling|Chomp}} can be used twice in a row. {{Int|Darkling|Nip}} cannot be used three times in a row.

After its HP reaches 0, its current intent changes to {{Int|Darkling|Regrowing...}} and cannot be targeted. On the next turn it will use {{Int|Darkling|Reincarnate}}.

== Interactions ==
* Cards with the {{KW|Fatal}} modifier only activate their effects whenever the last Darkling is killed.
