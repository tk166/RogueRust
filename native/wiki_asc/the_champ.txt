=== Phase One ===
Always uses {{Int|The Champ|Taunt}} every 4 turns.

'''On all other turns:'''
* {{Int|The Champ|Defensive Stance}} 15% chance
* {{Int|The Champ|Gloat}} ({{Int|The Champ|Defensive Stance}} {{Asc|19}}) 15% chance
* {{Int|The Champ|Face Slap}} 25% chance
* {{Int|The Champ|Heavy Slash}} 45% chance

No move can be used twice in a row. When he attempts to use the same move again, he will instead use the next move in the list above. For example, if he used {{Int|The Champ|Gloat}}, there's a 0% chance of him using {{Int|The Champ|Gloat}} again but a 40% chance of him using {{Int|The Champ|Face Slap}}. If he used {{Int|The Champ|Heavy Slash}}, the chance of {{Int|The Champ|Heavy Slash}} will be transferred to {{Int|The Champ|Face Slap}} instead (0% chance of {{Int|The Champ|Heavy Slash}}, 70% chance of {{Int|The Champ|Face Slap}})

On {{Asc|19|Ascension}}, when {{Int|The Champ|Gloat}} is replaced with {{Int|The Champ|Defensive Stance}}, if the last move was also {{Int|The Champ|Defensive Stance}}, he will instead use {{Int|The Champ|Gloat}}.

=== Phase Two ===
When his HP drops below 50%, he will use {{Int|The Champ|Anger}} on the next turn and enter Phase Two.

After using {{Int|The Champ|Anger}} he will use {{Int|The Champ|Execute}} the turn after.

He will then repeat this pattern for the rest of combat:
* Random Move (Same chances as Phase One)
* Random Move (Same chances as Phase One)
* {{Int|The Champ|Execute}}
