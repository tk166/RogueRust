'''Blue Slaver''' is a normal [[Monsters|Enemy]] found in Act 1 and Act 2. They can also appear in [[Elite]] combats and {{E|The Colosseum}} [[Event]].

Each turn, '''Blue Slaver''' has a 60% chance to use {{Int|Blue Slaver|Stab}} and a 40% chance to use {{Int|Blue Slaver|Rake}}. The same attack cannot be used three times in a row.

On {{Asc|17|Ascension}}, '''Blue Slaver''' will never use {{Int|Blue Slaver|Rake}} twice in a row.

'''Red Slaver''' is a normal [[Monsters|Enemy]] found in Act 1 and Act 2. They can also appear in [[Elite]] combats and {{E|The Colosseum}} [[Event]].

'''Red Slaver''' will always start the battle with {{Int|Red Slaver|Stab}}. If '''Red Slaver''' has not used {{Int|Red Slaver|Entangle}} yet, it has a 25% chance to be used each turn. '''Red Slaver''' will repeat the pattern of {{Int|Red Slaver|Scrape}}, {{Int|Red Slaver|Scrape}}, {{Int|Red Slaver|Stab}} until {{Int|Red Slaver|Entangle}} is used. After using {{Int|Red Slaver|Entangle}}, '''Red Slaver''' has a 55% chance of using {{Int|Red Slaver|Stab}} and a 45% chance of using {{Int|Red Slaver|Scrape}}. '''Red Slaver''' cannot use the same attack three times in a row, and can only use {{Int|Red Slaver|Entangle}} once per combat.

On {{Asc|17|Ascension}}, '''Red Slaver''' will repeat the pattern of {{Int|Red Slaver|Scrape}}, {{Int|Red Slaver|Stab}} until {{Int|Red Slaver|Entangle}} is used. After {{Int|Red Slaver|Entangle}} is used, '''Red Slaver''' cannot use {{Int|Red Slaver|Scrape}} twice in a row.

== Trivia ==

* Blue Slaver appears in the artwork of {{C|Blood for Blood}}, {{C|Disarm}}, {{C|Rampage}}, {{C|Outmaneuver}}, {{C|Sneaky Strike}} and {{C|Choke}}.
* Red Slaver appears in the artwork of {{C|Headbutt}} and {{C|Rampage}}.
