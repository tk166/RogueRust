== Hints ==
* This fight is a race to kill Hexaghost before turn 9. Before that, Hexaghost isn't very threatening, prefering to fire low damage {{Int|Hexaghost|Sear|Sears}} and {{Int|Hexaghost|Tackle|Tackles}}. However, on the 9th turn, it will use {{Int|Hexaghost|Inferno}}, which deals 24 ({{Asc|4|30}}, {{Asc|19|36}}) damage and fills your discard pile with {{C|Burn|Burns+}}. It's very hard to survive this attack, let alone the turns after.
** To kill Hexaghost in 9 turns, on average you need to deal around 30 damage per turn. Achieving this requires some sort of scaling, such as {{BD|Strength}} or {{BD|Poison}}.

* On the 2nd turn, Hexaghost uses {{Int|Hexaghost|Divider}}, the only attack in the game that scales with your current HP. If you are already low on health, this attack deals pitiful damage. Therefore, it's often preferable to '''not''' rest before this fight, and instead upgrade a card that will help you beat Hexaghost before {{Int|Hexaghost|Inferno}}.
** For example, if you have between 12 and 23 HP, {{Int|Hexaghost|Divider}} deals only 12 damage, easily blocked.
