* Has a 50% chance to use {{Int|Looter|Lunge}} or 50% chance to use {{Int|Looter|Smoke Bomb}}.
** If {{Int|Looter|Lunge}} is used on its third turn, it will then use {{Int|Looter|Smoke Bomb}} next.
* The turn after {{Int|Looter|Smoke Bomb}} has been used, it will {{Int|Looter|Escape}}.
