== Gremlin Wizard ==
{{Enemy Infobox
| Name  = Gremlin Wizard
| Link  = Gremlins#Gremlin Wizard
| Image = GremlinWizard.png
| HP    = 21-25
| AscHP = 22-26
| Debut = Act 1
| Type  = Normal
}}
'''Gremlin Wizards''' charge up before unleashing a powerful attack.

=== Pattern ===
{{Intents Table/start|Gremlin Wizard}}
{{Intents Table/row
| Name   = Charging
| Intent = Unknown
| Effect = Does nothing.
}}
{{Intents Table/row
| Name   = Ultimate Blast
| Intent = Attack6
| Effect = Deals (25;{{Asc|2|30}}) damage.
}}
{{Intents Table/end}}
'''Gremlin Wizard''' will always start by using {{Int|Gremlin Wizard|Charging}} twice in a row, then using {{Int|Gremlin Wizard|Ultimate Blast}}. After that and for the rest of combat, '''Gremlin Wizard''' will use {{Int|Gremlin Wizard|Charging}} three times in a row before using {{Int|Gremlin Wizard|Ultimate Blast}}.

On {{Asc|17|Ascension}}, '''Gremlin Wizard''' will use {{Int|Gremlin Wizard|Charging}} twice in a row, then use {{Int|Gremlin Wizard|Ultimate Blast}} every turn for the rest of combat.
{{Clear}}

== In Party With ==
=== Act 1 ===
In the '''Gremlin Gang''' encounter, randomly choosing from the following list for a total of 4 enemies:
* 2 {{M|Fat Gremlin|Fat Gremlins}}
* 2 {{M|Sneaky Gremlin|Sneaky Gremlins}}
* 2 {{M|Mad Gremlin|Mad Gremlins}}
* 1 {{M|Shield Gremlin}}
* 1 {{M|Gremlin Wizard}}

=== Act 2 ===
As {{BD|Minion|Minions}} in an Elite encounter consisting of:
* 2 random '''Gremlins'''
* 1 {{M|Gremlin Leader}}

= Tips =
* Gremlins summoned by {{M|Gremlin Leader}} will always have the {{BD|Minion}} [[Buff]], so the effects of {{KW|Fatal}} will not work on them.

= Trivia =
* The {{E|Wheel of Change}} and {{E|Match and Keep}} [[Events]] are run by Gremlins.
* {{R|Gremlin Visage}} is a reference to Gremlins, although it doesn't resemble any of the types seen in the game.
** {{R|Gremlin Horn}} also references Gremlins, specifically {{M|Gremlin Nob}}.
* Mad Gremlin appears in the artwork of {{C|Riddle with Holes}} and {{C|Trip}}.
* An unidentified Gremlin appears in the artwork of {{C|Cleave}}, {{C|Hemokinesis}}, {{C|Reckless Charge}}, {{C|Sever Soul}}, {{C|Eviscerate}}, {{C|Noxious Fumes}}, {{C|Rip and Tear}}, {{C|Bowling Bash}} and {{C|Expunger}}.
