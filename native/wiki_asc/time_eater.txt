'''Time Eater''' will always use {{Int|Time Eater|Haste}} when it is reduced to below half HP. {{Int|Time Eater|Haste}} can only be used once per combat. '''Time Eater''' will use one of the following three attacks each turn:
* {{Int|Time Eater|Reverberate}} (45% chance)
* {{Int|Time Eater|Head Slam}} (35% chance)
* {{Int|Time Eater|Ripple}} (20% chance)
'''Time Eater''' will not use {{Int|Time Eater|Reverberate}} three times in a row, or {{Int|Time Eater|Head Slam}}/{{Int|Time Eater|Ripple}} twice in a row.

== Trivia ==
* When playing as the [[Ironclad]], [[Silent]], or [[Defect]], Time Eater will say ''"Ah... company."'' on its first turn.
** When playing as the [[Watcher]], Time Eater will instead say ''"Never... Liked... You..."''.
