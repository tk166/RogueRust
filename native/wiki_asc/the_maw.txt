== Trivia ==
* The Maw is the only enemy whose Max HP does not change based on [[Ascension]] level.
* The Maw appears in the artwork of {{C|Weave}}.
* The Maw is likely an adult of the [[Jaw Worm]], from appearing in the beta art of [[Evolve]]. this is supported by Jaw worms living in act three, and one of the Jaw worm move being called grow.
