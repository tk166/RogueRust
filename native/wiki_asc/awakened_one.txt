=== Phase One ===
Always starts with {{Int|Awakened One|Slash}}.

'''Every turn after:'''
* {{Int|Awakened One|Soul Strike}} 25% chance
* {{Int|Awakened One|Slash}} 75% chance

{{Int|Awakened One|Slash}} cannot be used three times in a row. {{Int|Awakened One|Soul Strike}} cannot be used twice in a row.

=== Phase Two ===
Once its HP reaches 0, it loses {{BD|Curiosity}} and its intent changes to {{Int|Awakened One|Rebirth}}. Any {{BD|Strength}} gained from {{BD|Curiosity}} is kept.

Always starts Phase Two with {{Int|Awakened One|Dark Echo}}. Afterward, it has a 50/50 chance to use {{Int|Awakened One|Tackle}} or {{Int|Awakened One|Sludge}}. It cannot use either of these moves three times in a row.

The remaining {{M|Cultist|Cultists}} will flee when the Awakened One is defeated in Phase Two.

== Trivia ==
* The {{M|Cultist|Cultists}} in this fight are not {{BD|Minion|Minions}} despite fleeing when the Awakened One is defeated so {{KW|Fatal}} effects will still trigger when killing them.
* {{R|Matryoshka}} and {{R|Eternal Feather}} both reference this boss in their flavor text.
* According to {{R|Bird-Faced Urn|Bird-Faced Urn's}} flavor text, the Awakened One is the crow god Mazaleth.
** Fittingly, the {{R|Bird-Faced Urn}} has an effect triggered by playing {{QueryLink|Cards|type:power|Power}} cards, just like the Awakened One's {{BD|Curiosity}}.
* A {{C|Ritual Dagger}} is stuck in its chest.
