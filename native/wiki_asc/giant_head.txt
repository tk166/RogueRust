For the first 4 ({{Asc|18|3}}) turns, it has a 50/50 chance to use either {{Int|Giant Head|Count}} or {{Int|Giant Head|Glare}}. Cannot use either move three times in a row.

Afterwards, it repeats {{Int|Giant Head|It Is Time}} for the rest of combat.
