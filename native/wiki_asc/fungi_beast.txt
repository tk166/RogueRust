'''Fungi Beast''' is a normal [[Monsters|Enemy]] that appears in [[Act 1]] and [[Act 2]]. It also appears in the {{E|Hypnotizing Colored Mushrooms}} [[Event]] in groups of three.

'''Fungi Beast''' will use one of the following attacks each turn:
* {{Int|Fungi Beast|Bite}} (60% chance)
* {{Int|Fungi Beast|Grow}} (40% chance)
'''Fungi Beast''' will not use {{Int|Fungi Beast|Bite}} 3 times in a row or {{Int|Fungi Beast|Grow}} twice in a row.

As a hard pool encounter alongside:
* 1 {{M|Shelled Parasite}}

== Tips ==
* {{BD|Spore Cloud}} will still activate even if a '''Fungi Beast''' is killed during the enemy's turn (ex. from {{BD|Thorns}} damage). Make sure to keep that in mind to avoid the extra damage other enemies may inflict while you have {{BD|Vulnerable}}.

== Trivia ==
* '''Fungi Beasts''' appear to be [[Slay the Spire 2:Two-Tailed Rat|Two-tailed Rats]] infested with a parasitic fungus.
** {{M|Gremlin Nob}} wields a similar rodent-like skeleton as a weapon, presumably belonging to the same enemy.
