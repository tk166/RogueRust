== Hints ==
* Until '''Slime Boss''' {{Int|Slime Boss|Split|splits}}, {{Int|Slime Boss|Slam}} is the only way it can harm you. Other than this attack, it spends most of the fight doing nothing. There are many ways to deal with it other than with {{KW|Block}} and {{BD|Weak}}:
** As long as you can deal 70 ({{Asc|9|75}}) damage by turn 3, you can force '''Slime Boss''' to {{Int|Slime Boss|Split|split}} before it attacks.
** You may fully negate {{Int|Slime Boss|Slam}} with {{C|Buffer}}, {{R|Fossilized Helix}}, {{R|Incense Burner}} and {{P|Ghost in a Jar}}.
** Rest before the fight to have more than 35 ({{Asc|4|38}}) HP, then simply tank it. After all, you always heal after boss fights.
* {{Int|Slime Boss|Split}} creates two [[Slimes|Large Slimes]] with '''Slime Boss'''' remaining HP. Thus it's better to cause '''Slime Boss''' to lose as much health as possible before the {{Int|Slime Boss|Split|splitting}}.
** This can be done by bringing the boss as close as possible to half HP, then applying {{BD|Vulnerable}} and spending as much energy as possible playing Attacks.
** With {{KW|Wrath}}, the [[Watcher]] is particularly good at killing or nearly killing '''Slime Boss''' before it can {{Int|Slime Boss|Split|split}}.
* '''Slime Boss''' and its [[Slimes]] will clog your Deck with {{C|Slimed}} the longer the fight goes on, particularly harshly on higher [[Ascension|ascensions]].
** {{C|Evolve}} and {{C|Fire Breathing}} turn those {{C|Slimed}} into advantage.
* Sources of AOE damage like {{C|Whirlwind}} are effective against the [[Slimes]] that spawn after '''Slime Boss''' {{Int|Slime Boss|Split|Splits}}.

== Trivia ==

* '''Slime Boss''' appears in the artwork of {{C|Evolve}} and {{C|Grand Finale}}.
