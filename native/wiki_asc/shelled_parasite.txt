'''Shelled Parasite''' cannot use {{Int|Shelled Parasite|Fell}} on its first turn, except on {{Asc|17|Ascension}} when '''it''' will always start with {{Int|Shelled Parasite|Fell}}.

After that, it will use one of the following attacks each turn:
* {{Int|Shelled Parasite|Double Strike}} (40% chance)
* {{Int|Shelled Parasite|Life Suck}} (40% chance)
* {{Int|Shelled Parasite|Fell}} (20% chance)
'''Shelled Parasite''' cannot use {{Int|Shelled Parasite|Double Strike}} or {{Int|Shelled Parasite|Life Suck}} three times in a row, or {{Int|Shelled Parasite|Fell}} twice in a row. 

If all of its {{BD|Plated Armor}} is broken, '''Shelled Parasite''' will change its current [[Intent]] to {{Int|Shelled Parasite|Stunned}}. This can only happen once per combat.
