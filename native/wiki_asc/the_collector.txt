Always starts with {{Int|The Collector|Spawn}} and always uses {{Int|The Collector|Mega Debuff}} on turn 4.

Can only have two {{M|Torch Head|Torch Heads}} summoned at a time.

'''If there are 0-1 {{M|Torch Head|Torch Heads}} alive:'''
* {{Int|The Collector|Spawn}} 25% chance
* {{Int|The Collector|Fireball}} 45% chance
* {{Int|The Collector|Buff}} 30% chance

'''If both {{M|Torch Head|Torch Heads}} are alive:'''
* {{Int|The Collector|Fireball}} 70% chance
* {{Int|The Collector|Buff}} 30% chance

She cannot use {{Int|The Collector|Buff}} twice in a row and cannot use {{Int|The Collector|Fireball}} 3 times in a row.
