Always starts with {{Int|Chosen|Poke}} followed by {{Int|Chosen|Hex}}. After that, repeats the following pattern for the rest of the combat:
#{{Int|Chosen|Debilitate}} (50% chance) or {{Int|Chosen|Drain}} (50% chance)
#{{Int|Chosen|Poke}} (60% chance) or {{Int|Chosen|Zap}} (40% chance)

On {{Asc|17|Ascension}}, '''Chosen''' always starts with {{Int|Chosen|Hex}} instead of {{Int|Chosen|Poke}}.

== Lore ==
'''Chosen''' are implied to be connected to the {{M|Cultist|Cultists}} and {{M|Awakened One}} in some way, as they all share a blue crow motif.

== Trivia ==
* The '''Chosen''' is the only thing in the game that can apply {{BD|Hex}}.
* '''Chosen''' appears in the artwork of {{C|Immolate}}.
