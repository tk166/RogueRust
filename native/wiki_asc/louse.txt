The '''Red Louse''' is a normal [[Monsters|Enemy]] found exclusively in Act 1.

{{Intents Table/row
| Name   = Bite
| Intent = Attack2

}}
{{Intents Table/row
| Name         = Grow
| Intent       = Buff
| Effect       = Gains (3;{{Asc|17|4}}) {{BD|Strength}}.
| GivesToEnemy = Strength
}}
{{Intents Table/end}}
Has a 25% chance to use {{Int|Red Louse|Grow}} and a 75% chance of using {{Int|Red Louse|Bite}}. It cannot use the same move three times in a row.

On {{Asc|17|Ascension}} it cannot use {{Int|Red Louse|Grow}} any more than twice in a row.

=== Found With ===

===== Act 1 =====
In the Easy Pool along with:

* '''Red''' or '''Green Louse'''
* 2 '''Red''' or '''Green Louses'''
* {{M|Cultist}}
* {{M|Jaw Worm}}

In the Hard Pool with:

* 2 '''Red''' or '''Green Louses'''

Can be found as half of the '''Exordium Thugs''' encounter along with either:

* One of the [[Slavers]]
* One {{M|Cultist}}
* One {{M|Looter}}
Can be found as half of the '''Exordium Wildlife''' encounter with either:

* {{M|Fungi Beast}}
* {{M|Jaw Worm}}
